# Support

## How to get help

Sweet Hand is an open-source project with a small team of maintainers.

- **Questions about using or developing Sweet Hand:** search the
  [existing issues](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown/sweet-hand/issues) first,
  then open a new issue with the **Question** template.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the **Bug report** template.
- **Ideas and feature requests:** open an issue with the **Feature request**
  template.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do not open an issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, the answer may already be in:

- [README.md](README.md) for setup and commands
- [`backend/README.md`](backend/README.md) and
  [`frontend/README.md`](frontend/README.md) for each half of the stack
- [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/) for why things are the way they are

## Response times

Maintainers respond on a best-effort basis. There is no paid support.
