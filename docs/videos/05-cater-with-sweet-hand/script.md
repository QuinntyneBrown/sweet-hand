# 05 · Cater with Sweet Hand

Everybody asks who made the pelau. Your black cake is gone before the speeches start. Right now people find you through a cousin, a church group or a WhatsApp chat. Sweet Hand is a new way for them to find you.

## One place to be found

Sweet Hand opens soon in Toronto and the GTA. Jamaican, Trini, Guyanese, Bajan and more, all in one place, so people planning a birthday, a wedding or a christening can find someone with a sweet hand.

## Your own page

On launch day you get your own page. Your photos, your menu, the islands you cook from, the areas you serve and the size of event you take on. People see the areas you serve. We never ask for your home address.

## How it works

People browse by island, area and occasion. When they like what they see, they send an enquiry with their date and guest count. You reply, and you agree the details directly with them. Listing is free, and there are no fees.

## Be there on day one

To be listed you'll need a food handler certificate, and our team checks every page before it goes live. Joining the launch list takes a minute. Go to sweethand.ca, press Join as a caterer, add your business name and your email, and confirm the link we send you. We'll email you once, on launch day.
