Who qualifies

  • Cooks Caribbean food at home or at a small scale
  • Sells in Toronto or the GTA
  • Takes orders for events: not restaurants or food trucks, yet
  • Names their tradition: Jamaican, Trinidadian and Tobagonian, Guyanese, Bajan, Haitian…

Where they already sell

  • Instagram
  • Facebook groups and WhatsApp
  • Caribbean grocers and churches

Ask every yes

“Who else cooks that I should talk to?”

The pitch, in order

  1. How people find them now: a cousin, a church group, a WhatsApp chat
  2. Sweet Hand: by dish, island and area. Opens Thursday 26 November
  3. Free to list · they set the menu, batches and days · address private
The offer, word for word

The first 100 caterers who register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes, shot in our studio. In return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.

To confirm How “first 100” is counted, and the last day to book. Never promise a place.

The objections

“I sell on Instagram”

Keep it. Sweet Hand brings people who don't follow you yet.

“I don't have time”

Two minutes, together, now.

“Will people see my address?”

No. Areas served only; phone and email private.

“Do you deliver?”

Not at launch. Courier delivery is being explored.
