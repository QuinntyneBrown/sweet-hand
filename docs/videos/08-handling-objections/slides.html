Shop counter · church

"I don't have a licence. I can't be on a website."

Say

"The site never says anyone is certified. You write your own food safety note, in your words, or you write nothing. It's your call."

The rule

  • Never tell a cook they're allowed to sell
  • Never tell a cook they're not allowed
  • Keep two links ready: the public health unit's home-food guidance and the food handler course
  • Send them when asked, and leave it with them
Everywhere

"I don't want my address out there."

Say

"It never shows. You pick a neighbourhood or an intersection, and that's all anyone sees. Pickup details go only to the customer you choose, when you choose."

The line people repeat to their friends. Say it at every door.

People you know · church

"I only cook for people I know. I'm not a restaurant."

Say

"That's fine. You set the batches and the days. A page that says 'by pre-order, Fridays only, twenty plates' is exactly what the site is for."

Then ask for the dishes. Once they're describing the pelau, they're in.

Everywhere · shop owners

"So what's the catch? What does it cost?"

To the cook

"Nothing. Listing and searching stay free. Later there's an optional paid tier for cooks who want extras, and nobody has to take it."

To the shop owner

"The cooks are your best customers. A flyer at the till costs you nothing, and when they get found, they buy more from you."

Instagram · Facebook

"Who are you, though? I've never heard of Sweet Hand."

Say

"Fair. I'm a photographer and an engineer, and this is my own work. Here's the site, and here's a ninety-second video of what it does."

Show the preview and video 05 on your phone. A launch list today, a directory on November 26. "Will", not "is".

Instagram · WhatsApp

"Just send me something and I'll look at it."

Say

"Sure, sending it now. I'll check back with you Thursday."

  • Send the WhatsApp image and the link
  • Tracker: replied, not yes
  • Follow up in 48 hours; if the second message gets nothing, call
The church office

"We don't do business announcements during the service."

Say

"Understood. Could I speak to the hospitality committee, or the people who run the kitchen? And could I take a table after the service, or help at the bazaar?"

The announcement was never the point. The kitchen committee is.

The best cooks, in season

"It's the season. I'm swamped. Come back in January."

Say

"The list takes one minute, right now, on your phone. The photo shoot is ninety minutes whenever you choose. And the site opens before Christmas, so being on it is for this season."

Then do the minute with them: sweethand.ca, "Join as a caterer". Don't leave it for later.

Things to remember

  • A worry is not a no: answer in two sentences, then ask for the dishes
  • Never "allowed", never "not allowed": send the links, leave it with them
  • The address line is the one people repeat
  • "Send me something" is a maybe: put a date on the follow-up
  • One minute? Spend it on the list, not the pitch
