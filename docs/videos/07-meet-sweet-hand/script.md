# 07 · Meet Sweet Hand

Somebody you know has a sweet hand. The cousin whose pelau goes first. The auntie whose black cake is the reason you come. The friend who makes doubles on a Saturday morning.

## Find them

Sweet Hand is where Toronto and the GTA find them. Doubles, roti, oxtail, jerk, patties, pepper sauce, black cake, sorrel. Jamaican, Trini, Guyanese, Bajan, Haitian and more, all in one place.

## For cooks

If you have a sweet hand, this is for you. Your own page. Your menu, your islands, the areas you serve. Never your home address. Free to list, no fees.

## For the hungry

If you want good food, this is for you. Find a cook by dish, island and area. Send your date and your guest count, and agree it with them directly.

## Sign up

If you know someone with a sweet hand, send them here. Sweet Hand. Sign up free at sweethand.ca.
