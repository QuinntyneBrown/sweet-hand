In the mock's order, with the mock's copy (L2-093).

Only four kinds of link

The page links to

  • / and in-page anchors
  • The privacy notice, /privacy
  • The contact mailto: address

Never to

  • Sign in or registration
  • Caterer listings
  • Anything in the unopened product

L2-093 criterion 2, checked by e2e/specs/launch/serve-launch-site.spec.ts

Two ways to the form

  1. Get the launch email in the header: scroll to the form, focus its heading
  2. Join as a caterer: same, and select “A caterer” first, keeping any typed email

Small details that matter

  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce → jump, no smooth scroll
  • /#notify opens already at the form (the flyer's QR code can use it)
  • Focus lands on the section heading, so screen readers start in the right place

A slim application of its own

Frontend

frontend/projects/launch/

  • Its own build, serve, test and i18n targets
  • Imports only Angular and @sweet-hand/api; its only CSS is the design system
  • Deleted on launch day without touching the product

Backend

backend/src/launch-list/

  • An area of the existing NestJS API
  • No new stateful service: PostgreSQL only
  • Every route anonymous by explicit choice
