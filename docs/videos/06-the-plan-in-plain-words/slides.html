What if the founder cooks too?

A food handler certificate, and a kitchen listed as cook number 101.

never cook

What it takes

  1. Food handler course: about 7 hours, about $130 online at George Brown College, then a Toronto Public Health certificate
  2. Tell public health about the kitchen
  3. Jerk or curry: the full rules, including a separate hand-washing sink

Lighter path: low-risk food only, like bread. Course details as of October 2026; check before you sign up.
