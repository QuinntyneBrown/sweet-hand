# 08 · A pitch for caterers

Everybody asks who made the pelau. Your black cake is gone before the speeches start. But right now people only find you through a cousin, a church group or a WhatsApp chat.

## One place to be found

Sweet Hand is a new site where people in Toronto and the GTA find Caribbean caterers by dish, by island and by area. Jamaican, Trini, Guyanese, Bajan, Haitian and more, each one by name. It opens on Thursday the twenty-sixth of November, right before the Christmas orders.

## Your own page

You get your own page: your photos, your menu with dietary tags, the areas you serve and the size of event you take on. People send you an enquiry, and you agree the details with them directly.

It's free to list. You set the menu, the batches and the days. And your home address never shows, only the areas you serve.

## A free photoshoot

The first 100 caterers who register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes, shot in our studio. In return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.

## Ideas we're exploring

We're also exploring a few ideas for later, like delivery through a courier partner, and awards such as GTA's Best Jerk Chicken. They're ideas, not promises.

## Two things to do today

Go to sweethand.ca and press Join as a caterer. Add your business name and your own name, and you're on your way; it takes about two minutes. Then follow Sweet Hand on Facebook, so you hear about the launch and the photoshoot days first.

Sweet Hand. Your sweet hand, found.
