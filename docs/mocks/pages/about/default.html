Someone with a sweet hand

In the Caribbean, when someone’s cooking is just that good, people say they have a sweet hand. Toronto and the GTA are full of them: aunties, uncles and small kitchens feeding weddings, christenings, nine-nights and Sunday lunches.

Sweet Hand helps you find them. Browse by island, by area and by occasion, then send an enquiry with your date, guests and what you’d love to eat. The caterer replies here, and you decide together.

Free, and only enquiries

Listing and enquiring are free. No money moves through Sweet Hand; you agree price and payment directly with the caterer.

Every caterer is reviewed

Our team checks each profile and its food handler certificate before it goes live. Food safety statements are the caterer’s own; Sweet Hand does not inspect or certify kitchens.
