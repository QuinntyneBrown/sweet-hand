Privacy notice

Version 1.0 · in effect from Thursday 1 October 2026

Sweet Hand connects people planning events in Toronto and the GTA with Caribbean caterers. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and how to reach us. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: display name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash) and, for caterers, business name and phone. To sign you in and let caterers and customers reach each other.
  • Enquiries and messages: event date, guests, area, occasion and what you write. To pass your enquiry to the caterer you chose.
  • Postal codes: we keep only the first three characters, to match you with nearby caterers.
  • Food handler certificates: seen only by the caterer and our review team.
  • Visits: counted without cookies or third-party analytics, using a daily-changing code that can’t identify you.

Who sees it

Caterers see your display name and enquiry, never your email or phone unless you choose to share them in that conversation. Our operators see records only to review profiles and handle reports, and every look is logged. We never sell personal information.

We use two service providers. Northbound Mail Inc. sends our emails and processes them in Canada. Our bot check runs on servers in Canada. All of our data, backups and logs are stored in Canadian data centres.

Cookies

We only set the three cookies Sweet Hand needs to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is nothing to opt into.

Your choices

You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from your account page at any time. Deleted information is gone within 30 days, and from backups within 35.

Contact our privacy officer

Write to privacy@sweethand.example. If you aren’t satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
