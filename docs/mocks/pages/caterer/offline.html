Pelau House

  • Trinidadian & Tobagonian
  • Guyanese
  • Rated 4.7 · 23 reviews

Kendra Baptiste learned to burn sugar for pelau in her grandmother’s kitchen in Couva. Today she cooks the same one-pot pelau, curry goat and macaroni pie for weddings, christenings and big family Sundays across the east end.

Everything is cooked the morning of your event and arrives hot, with chafing dishes and serving spoons. Doubles and aloo pie are made to order for groups of 40 or more.
