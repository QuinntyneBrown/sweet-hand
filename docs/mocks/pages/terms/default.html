For everyone

For customers

  • An enquiry is a question, not a booking. Price, deposit and payment are agreed directly with the caterer.
  • You can review a caterer who replied to your enquiry, after your event, for up to 90 days.
  • Up to 10 enquiries a day keeps caterers’ inboxes free of spam.

For caterers
