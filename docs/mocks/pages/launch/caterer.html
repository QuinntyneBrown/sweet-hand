How it will work

1. Browse

Filter caterers by island, by area from Oshawa to Oakville, and by occasion. No account needed.

2. Enquire

Send your date, guest count and what you’d love to eat. The caterer replies in your Sweet Hand inbox.

3. Agree it together

Settle the menu and price directly with the caterer. No fees, and no money moves through Sweet Hand.
