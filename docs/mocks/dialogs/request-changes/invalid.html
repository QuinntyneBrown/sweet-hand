Auntie Bev’s Kitchen

  • Jamaican

Beverley Campbell has cooked for every christening, nine-night and graduation on her street in Oshawa for thirty years. Her brown stew chicken and curry goat are cooked low and slow, the way her mother did it in Mandeville.

Food arrives hot in foil trays with serving spoons. For repasts, Bev can cook the day before and deliver by 10 a.m.
