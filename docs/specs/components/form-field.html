Sizes

One size. The label, gap, help and counter are the same beside a 52 px .input--lg (sign-in) and a 44 px toolbar field; width is always the column.

Large control and medium control under the same label style. AC-1

States

Focus is forced on the control with data-state; every other state uses the real attributes the field and its control render.

Responsive

The field has no breakpoint rules; these frames show wrapping. At 320 px the help wraps and the counter drops beneath it, end-aligned; a long email address in help breaks rather than overflowing.
