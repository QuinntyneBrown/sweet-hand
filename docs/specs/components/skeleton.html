Sizes

No size scale. Text and title follow the surrounding font size (they are in em); circles fill their photo or avatar box; rects and pills take the control height they replace. AC-4, AC-9

States

Not interactive: the states are the loading lifecycle. Reduced motion is shown with an inline animation: none, as the product's media query does.
