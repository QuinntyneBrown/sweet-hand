Busy: required (D-5)

Busy sets aria-busy="true" and aria-disabled="true", keeps the label (transparent) so the width is unchanged, and is never dimmed. The specimens apply the D-5 rule with a specimen-only style; today's components.css would dim them to 50 %.

Required (D-5): resting and busy "Send enquiry" are the same size; the spinner is in the label colour at full opacity. AC-12, AC-13, AC-14, AC-15, AC-16, AC-29, AC-30

Responsive

Each frame is a real viewport. Below 48rem the small "Deactivate", the small icon-only "Move Doubles up" and the small "Show" grow to 44 px; at 768 px they are 36 px. The wrapping label wraps at 320 px and nothing scrolls sideways.
