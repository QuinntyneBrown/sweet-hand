Responsive

Avatars do not change across breakpoints, so no width frames: rows set their size, and the trigger stays 44 × 44 px at every width, including at 320 px and at 200 % zoom. Moving the customer's trigger into the site menu below 48rem is the site header's layout (see site-header.html).
