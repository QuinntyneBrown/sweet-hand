Grouped, named by the heading "Your privacy" (labelledBy)

What we collect, and why

Your name and email to run your account, and the event details you put in an enquiry so the caterer can reply.

Who sees it

A caterer sees your name and your enquiry, not your email or phone, until you choose to share them. Sharing contact details

How long we keep it

Enquiries and messages are kept for 3 years after the enquiry closes.

Sizes

One size. Every summary is at least --target-comfortable (44 px) high and full width; the quiet disclosure keeps the 44 px row in smaller muted type. The group's width is its container's. AC-17

States

Open and closed use the real open attribute; hover and focus are forced with data-state on the summary (documentation only). There is no disabled, loading or empty state (D-11).
