Variants

The product bar is fixed and hidden from 62rem, so this section uses .sticky-cta--static (documentation only, D-3) to keep it in the flow. The specimens sit in a padded canvas that is narrower than the viewport while the bar keeps the viewport's --layout-margin, so on a phone they truncate more than the real bar; the frames under Responsive show real widths with the real fixed bar.

Sizes

One size: a 44 px button plus --space-3 above and below (about 69 px) and the bottom safe area; always full width. AC-11

States

The bar has no states of its own; its button does. Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state; offline uses native disabled.
