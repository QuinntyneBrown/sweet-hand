Sizes

One weight, 1 px (--border-width-hairline), at every size. Horizontal and labelled dividers span their container with --space-6 above and below; the vertical divider stretches to its row with --space-3 each side.

States

Not interactive: no hover, focus or disabled state. Each variant on each surface it appears on.

Responsive

Required (D-5): below 36rem the vertical divider is not displayed, so it never dangles at the end of a wrapped row; at 600 px it is. The French label "Demandes fermées" fits between its rules at 320 px.
