# L1 - High-Level Requirements

**Product:** Sweet Hand
**Source:** `docs/Sweet Hand — L1 Requirements.md` (Oct 10, 2026)
**Status:** Approved 2026-10-10
**Last updated:** 2026-10-12 (L1-026 to L1-029 and assumptions A-9 to A-13, the operator directory of places and events, added as a draft, pending approval; L1-024, L1-025 and assumption A-8 added 2026-10-11 as a draft, pending approval)

Sweet Hand is a web platform that connects people in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) with people selling Caribbean food. The first release focuses on caterers: a customer browses a photo-led listing, opens a caterer's page, and sends an enquiry for an event.

Requirements L1-001 to L1-020 come directly from the source document (source ID `L1-NN` maps to `L1-0NN`). Requirements L1-021 to L1-023 are cross-cutting additions for production readiness. Requirements L1-024 and L1-025 cover the pre-launch site, which runs before the product opens; they come from the launch page mocks (`docs/mocks/pages/launch/`). Requirements L1-026 to L1-029 cover the operator directory of Caribbean food places and events; they come from the directory mocks (`docs/mocks/pages/admin-places/`, `admin-place/`, `admin-places-map/`, `admin-events/`, `admin-event/`, `admin-calendar/` and their dialogs).

## Roles

| Role | Who they are | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor | Anyone using the platform without signing in | Browse caterers |
| Customer | A signed-in person in Toronto or the GTA planning a meal or an event | Find a caterer they like and get in touch |
| Caterer | A person or small business selling Caribbean food | Be found, show their food, receive enquiries |
| Operator | The Sweet Hand team | Approve caterers, keep listings accurate, see how the platform is used |

## Assumptions (resolved open questions)

The source document lists open questions to settle before L2. The answers below were approved with this L1 set on 2026-10-10, and the L2 set is written against them. If an answer changes, the L2s listed against it must be revised.

| # | Question | Assumed answer | Affected L2s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-1 | Sellers: caterers only, or also home cooks, restaurants and market vendors? | Caterers only for the first release. | L2-037 |
| A-2 | Transaction: enquiry only, or booking and payment? | Enquiry only. No money moves through the platform. | L2-022 to L2-036 |
| A-3 | Revenue: free, subscription, or commission? | Free listing for the first release. No billing features. | None |
| A-4 | Food safety: must a caterer show a food handler certificate or inspection before approval? | Yes. A food handler certificate (number and expiry) is required to submit for approval. Only operators can see it. | L2-040, L2-047 |
| A-5 | Reviews: open to any customer, or only after a confirmed event? | Only a customer whose enquiry received a caterer reply and whose event date has passed. | L2-049 |
| A-6 | Languages: English only, or French as well? | English only for the first release. All user-facing text must be externalised so that French can be added without code changes. | L2-064 |
| A-7 | Data location | All personal information is stored in Canadian data centres. | L2-075 |
| A-8 | Pre-launch: may the platform email people before it opens? | Yes, once. A visitor may join a launch list with express consent under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), confirmed by email. Each confirmed address gets a single launch-day email, and the list is deleted 30 days after launch. This is not a newsletter. *Draft, pending approval; confirm the email rules against CASL with counsel.* | L2-092 to L2-107 |
| A-9 | Directory: who sees the directory of places and events? | Operators only, for the first release. The directory is curated by the Sweet Hand team; places and event organisers have no accounts, and nothing in the directory is shown to visitors or customers until a public directory is specified and mocked. "Listed" and "Published" mark what a later public directory would show. *Draft, pending approval.* | L2-110 to L2-136 |
| A-10 | Directory: how do places relate to caterers? | They are separate records. A place is a storefront business or a food truck; a caterer is an account under A-1. A home cook is never added as a place. *Draft, pending approval.* | L2-110, L2-133 |
| A-11 | Directory: which addresses may it hold? | A storefront's business address, which may be shown wherever the place is shown. A food truck has a home-base municipality only, never a street address, and its home base is never shown outside the operator console. An event venue is a venue name or an intersection, never a home address. *Draft, pending approval.* | L2-110, L2-116, L2-123, L2-133 |
| A-12 | Directory: are deletions permanent? | Yes, after a 10-second undo. "Mark closed" (places) and "Cancel event" (events) are the reversible alternatives that keep history. *Draft, pending approval.* | L2-118, L2-119, L2-128, L2-129 |
| A-13 | Directory: which map tiles and geocoder? | `<TO SUPPLY>`. Until one is chosen, the map draws a schematic of the GTA from stored coordinates. Whatever is chosen receives only business addresses, map coordinates and tile requests, never personal information, and needs no third-party script on any page other than the directory map and editors. *Draft, pending approval.* | L2-114, L2-120, L2-122 |

## Out of scope for the first release

- Online payment, deposits and invoicing
- Delivery logistics or driver dispatch
- Native iOS and Android apps
- Markets outside Toronto and the GTA
- Cuisines outside the Caribbean
- Marketing email and newsletters, other than the single launch-day email (A-8)
- A public directory of places and events, and accounts for places or event organisers (A-9)

## L1-001: Caterer listing
The platform shall present a listing of all published caterers.

## L1-002: Photo-led listing cards
The listing shall lead with food photography and show no more than a name, cuisine and area per caterer.

## L1-003: Filter by cuisine or island of origin
A customer shall be able to filter the listing by Caribbean cuisine or island of origin.

## L1-004: Filter and search by location
A customer shall be able to filter or search the listing by location within Toronto and the GTA.

## L1-005: Browsing without an account
A customer shall be able to browse the listing and caterer pages without an account.

## L1-006: Caterer page
Each caterer shall have a page showing photos, a description, menu or sample dishes, and the areas they serve.

## L1-007: Event sizes and occasions
Each caterer page shall state the event sizes and occasions the caterer takes on.

## L1-008: Send an enquiry
A customer shall be able to send an enquiry to a caterer with event date, guest count and location.

## L1-009: Enquiry notifications
The platform shall notify the caterer of each new enquiry and the customer of each reply.

## L1-010: Enquiry history
A customer and a caterer shall be able to view the history of their enquiries.

## L1-011: Caterer registration and profile creation
A caterer shall be able to register and create their own profile.

## L1-012: Caterer content management
A caterer shall be able to add, replace and remove their photos, menu and profile details.

## L1-013: Operator approval before publishing
A caterer profile shall be published only after operator approval.

## L1-014: Customer reviews
A customer who made an enquiry shall be able to leave a review of that caterer.

## L1-015: Reporting content
The platform shall let any user report a listing or review for operator attention.

## L1-016: Operator moderation
An operator shall be able to approve, edit, suspend and remove caterer profiles and reviews.

## L1-017: Usage metrics
An operator shall be able to see counts of caterers, visits and enquiries over time.

## L1-018: Phone, tablet and desktop support
The platform shall be usable on phone, tablet and desktop browsers.

## L1-019: Accessibility
The platform shall meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility.

## L1-020: Privacy
The platform shall handle personal information in line with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).

## L1-021: Security
The platform shall protect accounts, personal information and content against unauthorised access and tampering. This includes authentication, role-based and per-record authorisation, input validation, protection against the OWASP Top 10, and abuse prevention.

## L1-022: Performance
The platform shall load and respond quickly on mobile networks and stay fast as the number of caterers, visitors and enquiries grows.

## L1-023: Reliability and operability
The platform shall be available, observable and recoverable. The operations team shall be able to detect, diagnose and recover from failures without losing data customers or caterers have submitted.

## L1-024: Pre-launch site
Before the product opens, the platform shall present a single public page at its home address that says what Sweet Hand is, invites visitors and caterers to join a launch list, and runs as its own small application, separate from the product.

## L1-025: Launch list
A visitor shall be able to join a launch list with their email address and express consent, confirm it by email, remove themselves at any time without signing in, and receive one email when Sweet Hand opens, after which the list is deleted.

## L1-026: Directory of places
An operator shall be able to keep a directory of Caribbean food places in Toronto and the GTA — restaurants, takeout counters, grocery stores, bakeries and food trucks — adding, finding, editing, closing, reopening and deleting them, without ever recording a home cook's address.

## L1-027: Places map
An operator shall be able to see the directory's places on a map of Toronto and the GTA, filter them by type, and see which food trucks are out on a given day.

## L1-028: Directory of events
An operator shall be able to keep a directory of Caribbean food events in Toronto and the GTA — festivals, markets, trade shows, food truck stops, pop-ups and workshops — including repeating events, the directory places taking part, cancellations and deletions.

## L1-029: Event calendar
An operator shall be able to see the directory's events on a calendar by month, week and day, filter them by type, and add an event on a chosen day.
