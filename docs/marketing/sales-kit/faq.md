# FAQ for caterers

Written so a sales person can read each answer out loud. Every answer comes
from the repository (requirements in `docs/specs/`, the plans in `docs/plans/`
and the existing marketing copy); the source is in brackets for sales people
and is not read out. Anything the repository does not settle is marked
**to confirm**: say "I'll check and come back to you", never a guess.

The two asks, at the end of every conversation: **register** at
`sweethand.ca` (Join as a caterer) and **follow** the Facebook Page at
<https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595065212396>.

## What does it cost?

Nothing. Listing on Sweet Hand is free and there are no fees. No money moves
through the site: people send you an enquiry and you agree the price with them
directly. Later there may be an optional paid tier for caterers who want extra
tools; nobody has to take it, and the listing stays free.
[L1 assumptions A-2 and A-3; business plan; maker recruitment plan]

## What does my page show?

Your own page shows, in this order: your photos (3 to 30, each with a
caption), your business name, the traditions you cook from, a description in
your words, the size of events you take on and the occasions you cater, your
menu, the areas you serve, your reviews, and a "Send an enquiry" button.
People find you in the caterer listing by tradition, area and occasion.
[L2-015, L2-008, L2-011, L2-021]

Each tradition is named on its own: Jamaican, Trinidadian and Tobagonian,
Guyanese, Barbadian (Bajan), Haitian, Grenadian, Vincentian, St. Lucian and
more. You pick up to five. [L2-007, L2-039]

## Will people see my address?

No. Your page shows the areas you serve, such as Scarborough, Brampton or
Ajax, grouped by region. We never ask for your home address. Your phone
number and email stay private too: they are shared with a customer only if
you choose to share them in that one conversation.
[L2-010, L2-035; AGENTS.md domain rules]

## How do allergens and dietary tags work?

Every dish can carry tags from one fixed list: Vegetarian, Vegan,
Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Halal, Contains nuts, Contains shellfish and Spicy.
They show as labels on the dish, not buried in the description, so people with
an allergy can see them at a glance. You choose the tags for your own dishes.
[L2-017]

## Who sets the batches, the days and the pre-order cut-offs?

You do. Your menu, the size of event you take on and the dates you accept are
yours; you decide which enquiries to take. At launch, people send an enquiry
with their date and guest count and you agree the details, the quantity and
the cut-off directly with them. Pre-orders with batch limits, cut-offs and
"sold out" are planned for after launch; the date is **to confirm**.
[L2-020, L2-022; launch roadmap, "Out until after launch"]

## Do you deliver?

Not at launch. You agree pickup or delivery with each customer yourself, the
way you do now. **We're exploring** delivery through a courier partner for
later. It is an idea, not a feature, and there is no date.
[business plan, "Stage 3" and "Delivery"]

## Will there be awards?

**We're exploring** ideas like awards, for example "GTA's Best Jerk Chicken".
It is a coming idea, not a feature: there are no categories, rules or dates
yet. Follow the Facebook Page to hear first. [not in the plans yet; to confirm]

## Does Sweet Hand certify my kitchen?

No. Sweet Hand does not certify, inspect or approve kitchens. To be listed you
add a food handler certificate (holder, issuer, number and expiry); only you
and the Sweet Hand team see it. Our team checks every page before it goes
live, but that check is not a kitchen inspection. Whether you can sell food
from home is between you and your public health unit.
[L1 assumption A-4, L2-040, L2-046; design-system content rules]

## How do I register?

Go to `sweethand.ca` and press **Join as a caterer**.

- **Before 26 November**, that adds you to the launch list as a caterer: you
  confirm the link we email you, and we email you once, on launch day.
  [L2-094 to L2-101]
- **From 26 November**, it opens caterer registration: business name, your
  name, email, phone and a password. Then you build your page as you go: it
  saves itself, works on a phone, and a checklist shows what is left. When
  it is complete you submit it, and our team reviews it within 3 business
  days. [L2-037 to L2-039]
- Founding caterers may be invited to build their page before launch day.
  That is planned, not built; **to confirm.** [launch roadmap, SH-02 to SH-10]

To submit your page you need: a description, your traditions and areas, the
size of events you take, at least one occasion, 3 to 30 photos with captions,
3 to 50 dishes and a current food handler certificate. [L2-039]

## For sales people

This section is for the sales team only. Do not read it out or send it.

### Who qualifies

A caterer qualifies when all of these are true:

- They cook Caribbean food at home or at a small scale, and say which
  tradition in their own words. "Caribbean" alone is not an answer: ask.
- They sell in Toronto or the GTA (the 30 areas in L2-010).
- They cater: they take orders for events of some size. The first release is
  for caterers only (L1 assumption A-1). Restaurants and food trucks do not
  qualify yet.
- They have, or will get, a food handler certificate. It is required to
  submit a page (A-4). Never tell them they are or are not allowed to sell.

### How to log a lead

Log every conversation the same day in the founder's private tracker, with
only these columns (maker recruitment plan, "What to collect from each maker"):

```text
date, name, business name, phone or whatsapp, instagram, tradition, dishes,
area, how they sell now, food handler note (their words, optional),
status (lead / contacted / replied / yes / shoot booked / on launch list),
referred by, referred (names they gave), next step, next step date
```

- Record the **area**, never a home address.
- Add **facebook follow: yes / no** in the next step column until the tracker
  has its own column (to confirm).
- Ask every yes for two more names: "Who else cooks that I should talk to?"
- The tracker is personal data. Never share it or copy it to your own phone.

### What never to promise

- That Sweet Hand certifies, inspects or approves a kitchen, or that a listing
  is a licence.
- That they will get orders, or how many.
- Delivery, awards, pre-orders or payments as features. Say "we're
  exploring". Only the listing, enquiries and reviews exist at launch.
- A photoshoot place before the founder confirms how the first 100 are
  counted. Say "the first 100 registrations get it, and I'll confirm your place".
- A date for anything except the launch on Thursday 26 November 2026.
- That their page will be approved: our team reviews every page.
- Anything about their address: it never shows, and you never write it down.
