# Caterer sales kit

Everything a Sweet Hand sales person needs to sign up Caribbean caterers and
home cooks in Toronto and the GTA. Every piece asks for two things:

1. **Register** on Sweet Hand: `https://sweethand.ca`, then **Join as a caterer**.
2. **Follow** the Sweet Hand Facebook Page:
   <https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595065212396>
   (recorded in [`../facebook/README.md`](../facebook/README.md#published)).

The offer, word for word, in every piece:

> **The first 100 caterers who register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes,
> shot in our studio. In return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.**

Delivery and awards appear only as **ideas we're exploring**, never as features.
Sweet Hand opens on **Thursday 26 November 2026**.

## In a conversation, step by step

1. **Before you go**, watch [video 09](../../videos/09-sales-training/README.md)
   once, learn the [30-second pitch](elevator-pitch.md#30-seconds-spoken),
   and keep the [FAQ](faq.md) open on your phone. Carry leave-behinds and
   photoshoot cards.
2. **Check they qualify** ([FAQ, "Who qualifies"](faq.md#who-qualifies)):
   Caribbean food, cooked at home or at a small scale, in Toronto or the GTA,
   for events. Ask which tradition, and write down their words.
3. **Pitch.** Say the 30-second version, or the 60-second one if they have
   time. On a laptop or tablet, walk the deck; on a phone, play
   [video 08](../../videos/08-pitch-for-caterers/README.md) or show the
   screenshots.
4. **Say the offer word for word** (above). If they ask how "first 100" is
   counted or the last day to book, say "I'll confirm your place"; never
   promise one.
5. **Answer objections** from the [table](elevator-pitch.md#objections) and
   the FAQ. Anything not there: "I'll check and come back to you."
6. **Ask 1: register now**, on their phone. `sweethand.ca` → **Join as a
   caterer**. Before 26 November that puts them on the launch list as a
   caterer: business name, email, then they confirm the emailed link. From
   26 November it opens caterer registration (`screenshots/register-caterer-phone.png`).
7. **Ask 2: follow the Facebook Page**, with them, before you leave. Scan the
   QR on the leave-behind or the deck's slide 12.
8. **Leave something**: the leave-behind or the photoshoot card. If you met
   online, send the [WhatsApp text](elevator-pitch.md#text-or-whatsapp) and
   `flyer/sweet-hand-caterer-share.png`.
9. **Log the lead the same day** ([FAQ, "How to log a lead"](faq.md#how-to-log-a-lead)),
   including whether they followed on Facebook. Areas only, never addresses.
10. **Follow up in 48 hours** if they said "send me something".

## The photoshoot terms

Settled by the plans:

- **What:** a free studio shoot of 10 dishes, about 90 minutes in the
  downtown Toronto studio. The caterer brings the food and keeps the photos
  for their own Instagram (`docs/plans/business-plan.md`, "Months 1–3: seed
  the supply"; `docs/plans/maker-recruitment.md`, "The pitch").
- **Who:** the first 100.
- **In return:** a complete profile and a launch-day post (business plan).

**To confirm** by the founder before anyone promises a place:

- How "first 100" is counted: launch-list sign-ups as a caterer, accounts
  created from 26 November, or the recruitment tracker's dated yes (the
  maker recruitment plan counts a maker once they said yes and are on the
  launch list, or a sales person logged it).
- The last day to register for it, and the last shoot day.
- How shoot days are booked, and by whom.
- Where the launch-day post goes (the caterer's own Instagram or Facebook,
  or a post with Sweet Hand), and whether Sweet Hand may use the photos too.

## Regenerating

Run from the repository root with Node.js 22 (`frontend/` installed, which
provides Playwright and Chromium).

```sh
# 1. Screenshots: the backend in test mode and the product app, as the e2e suite runs them
(cd backend && PORT=4300 TEST_CONTROL=1 npm start &)
(cd frontend && E2E_API_PORT=4300 npx ng serve sweet-hand --port 4200 &)
node docs/marketing/sales-kit/screenshots/capture.mjs        # --only register-caterer for one

# 2. Flyers and the Facebook QR code (with the rest of the marketing kit, or alone)
node docs/marketing/render.mjs --only sales-kit

# 3. Deck, PDF and contact sheet (needs LibreOffice `soffice` and Poppler `pdftoppm`)
npm install --prefix .cache/deck pptxgenjs@3.12.0           # once; .cache/ is ignored
node docs/marketing/sales-kit/deck/build.mjs

# 4. Videos: see docs/videos/08-pitch-for-caterers/README.md and 09-sales-training/README.md
```

`capture.mjs` uses its own test-mode environment (`x-test-session`), seeded
with the fictional cast and the mocks' clock (Saturday 10 October 2026). It
fails if a page shows an email address, a phone number or a street address.
The deck reads the screenshots, so capture before building it.

## Decisions and open questions

Decisions made while building the kit:

- **Screenshots come from the real product app**, not the mocks: every screen
  rendered with the seeded cast, so no mock was needed.
- **Jerk chicken.** No caterer in the seed cast lists jerk chicken. Rather than
  change the seed (production code), `capture.mjs` and video 08's clips sign in
  as Beverley Campbell (Auntie Bev's Kitchen, Jamaican, the e2e `beverley`
  scenario) and add "Jerk chicken" through the menu editor, in a throwaway
  test environment. Pelau House stays the main profile because it has reviews.
- **A duplicate slug in the test cast.** The seed's moderation cast already
  holds a pending "Auntie Bev's Kitchen" on the same slug as the `beverley`
  scenario's, so her public page did not resolve. The scripts rename the
  pending one first with the existing `rename-caterer` scenario. Worth fixing
  in the test cast separately.
- **"Register" before launch means the launch list.** Production is in
  pre-launch mode until 26 November (`docs/plans/launch-roadmap.md`), so
  "Join as a caterer" adds a caterer to the launch list until then, and opens
  caterer registration from launch day. The kit says both; it does not
  promise founding-caterer invites (SH-02 to SH-10), which are planned, not built.
- **Product links show `app.sweethand.ca`** in the recordings (ADR 0016); every
  printed link and QR is `https://sweethand.ca`, as in the existing kit.
- **The deck uses Arial**, not Figtree: a `.pptx` cannot carry its font, and
  Arial looks the same wherever it opens. Colours are the design system's and
  the logo is drawn in Figtree. The flyers use Figtree, as the existing flyer does.
- **Paper sizes.** The leave-behind is US Letter, so it prints at home or in
  any office; the photoshoot card is half Letter, two to a sheet. Both reuse
  `../assets/print.css` and `../lib/press.mjs`.
- **Narration** was synthesized offline with Kokoro (`af_heart`), as for
  video 05, because the Edge read-aloud service was blocked from the build
  machine.
- **The caption file** for video 08 is cut from the narration's timing
  manifest, the way the builder cuts burned-in captions.

Open questions for the founder:

1. **Delivery conflicts with the business plan.** The plan says Sweet Hand
   "will not be ... a delivery company", while its Stage 3 plans "delivery
   through a courier API, never a Sweethand fleet". The kit frames delivery as
   "we're exploring delivery through a courier partner". Confirm that wording,
   or drop delivery from the kit.
2. **Awards are not in any plan.** "GTA's Best Jerk Chicken" appears only as
   an idea being explored. Confirm Sweet Hand wants to mention awards at all.
3. **The photoshoot terms** marked "to confirm" above: how "first 100" is
   counted, the deadline, booking, the launch-day post and photo use.
4. **A Facebook follow column** in the recruitment tracker; until then sales
   people note it in "next step".
5. **Photo licences.** The food photos inside the screenshots are the mocks'
   images (`docs/mocks/assets/img/`), also used in video 05. Confirm their
   licence before printing the leave-behind in volume.
6. **The founding-caterer invite** (SH-02 to SH-10): once it is built, step 6
   above and the FAQ's "How do I register?" can tell caterers to build their
   page before launch.

Deferred: building delivery or awards (they need L1/L2, a design and mocks
first), any change to registration or tracking photoshoot eligibility in the
app, posting, printing or sending the kit, and French or Haitian Creole
versions.
