# Elevator pitch

For Sweet Hand sales people talking to Caribbean caterers and home cooks in
Toronto and the GTA. Every conversation asks for two things:

1. **Register** on Sweet Hand: `sweethand.ca`, then **Join as a caterer**.
2. **Follow** the Sweet Hand Facebook Page:
   <https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595065212396>.

Say it in your own words. Keep the facts exactly as written here and in the
[FAQ](faq.md). Lines marked *to confirm* wait on the founder; never fill them in
yourself.

## The offer, in one sentence

Use this sentence wherever the photoshoot comes up, word for word:

> **The first 100 caterers who register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes,
> shot in our studio. In return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.**

How "first 100" is counted and the last day to book are *to confirm* (see the
[FAQ](faq.md#the-free-photoshoot)).

> You know how people find you now: a cousin, a church group, a WhatsApp chat.
> Sweet Hand is a new site where people in Toronto and the GTA find Caribbean

> November, right before the Christmas orders.
>
> It's free to list. You set the menu, the batches and the days. Your home
> address never shows, only the area you serve. And the first 100 caterers who
> register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes, shot in our studio. In
> return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.
>
> Can we sign you up now? It takes two minutes. And follow us on Facebook so
> you see the launch.

## Text or WhatsApp

Send as one message. Typed links are tappable; links inside images are not.

```text
Hi [name], it's [your name] from Sweet Hand. Sweet Hand is a new site where people in Toronto and the GTA find Caribbean caterers by dish, island and area. It opens Thursday 26 November.

Free to list. You set the menu, the batches and the days. Your home address never shows.

The first 100 caterers who register get a free photoshoot: 10 of their dishes, shot in our studio. In return we ask for a complete profile and a launch-day post.

1. Sign up: https://sweethand.ca → Join as a caterer
2. Follow us: https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595065212396
```

A shorter follow-up, 48 hours later, if they have not signed up:

```text
Hi [name], just checking in. The free photoshoot is for the first 100 caterers who register, so I didn't want you to miss it. Sign up at https://sweethand.ca (Join as a caterer) and follow us at https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595065212396. Any questions, ask me here.
```

## Objections

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "I already sell on Instagram." | "Keep it. Sweet Hand is where people go looking for a caterer by dish and area, so it brings people who don't follow you yet. You can send your Instagram followers to your page too, and the photos from the shoot are yours to post." |
| "I don't have time." | "Signing up takes two minutes, and I can do it with you now. The page saves as you go, so you can add photos and dishes later, from your phone. The shoot gives you the photos." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Nothing. Listing is free and there are no fees. Later there may be an optional paid tier; nobody has to take it." |
| "Will people see my address?" | "No. Your page shows the areas you serve, like Scarborough or Brampton, never your address. Your phone and email stay private until you choose to share them with someone who enquired." |
| "I only cook for people I know." | "That's fine. You set the batches and the days, and you decide which enquiries you take." |
| "Do you deliver?" | "Not at launch. You agree pickup or delivery with each customer yourself. Delivery through a courier partner is something we're exploring, not a promise." |
| "Do I need a licence?" | "Sweet Hand doesn't certify or inspect kitchens. To be listed you add a food handler certificate; only our team sees it. Whether you can sell from home is between you and your public health unit." |
| "Send me something." | Send the text above and the [flyer](flyer/) image. Follow up in 48 hours: "send me something" is a maybe, not a yes. |

## Close

End every conversation with both asks, in this order:

> "Let's get you signed up: sweethand.ca, Join as a caterer. And while we're
> here, can you follow Sweet Hand on Facebook? That's where the launch and the
> photoshoot days get announced."

Then log the lead (see [For sales people](faq.md#for-sales-people)).
