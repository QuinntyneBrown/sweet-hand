# Instagram

Everything needed to open the Sweet Hand Instagram account and make its first
posts. Instagram needs its own account (username, email, password), so a person
opens it; this folder holds the settings and the copy. The images come from the
Facebook kit, which is already Instagram's sizes.

## The account

| Setting | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Username | `sweethand`, or `sweethandtoronto` to match the YouTube handle if that is taken |
| Name | Sweet Hand |

| Bio | See below (under 150 characters) |

| Contact email | `hello@sweethand.ca` (the production mailbox in `docs/operations/`), shown as a contact button |

## First posts

Post them in this order, a day apart. Links in captions are not tappable on
Instagram, so each caption ends with "link in bio".

**1 · For eaters**, with `post-1-eaters.png`:

```text
Someone in Toronto has a sweet hand, and you're about to find them.

Sweet Hand is a new place to find the Caribbean home cooks and small-batch food makers of Toronto and the GTA: Jamaican, Trinidadian, Guyanese, Barbadian, Haitian, Grenadian and more, by dish, island and area.

Opening soon. Join the launch list, link in bio.

#Toronto #TorontoFood #CaribbeanFood #GTA #Jamaican #Trini #Guyanese #Bajan #Doubles #Roti #Oxtail #Jerk
```

**2 · For makers**, with `post-2-makers.png`:

```text
Cook from home or cater small events in Toronto or the GTA? Let Toronto find you.

On launch day you get your own page on Sweet Hand: your photos, your menu, the islands you cook from and the areas you serve. Listing is free, there are no fees, and we never ask for your home address.

Join the launch list as a caterer, link in bio.

#Toronto #TorontoFood #CaribbeanFood #Caterer #HomeCook #SmallBatch #GTA #Scarborough #Brampton #Mississauga #Ajax #Pickering
```

**3 · The promo video**: post `docs/videos/05-cater-with-sweet-hand/05-cater-with-sweet-hand.mp4`
as a Reel (1:19, 16:9; Instagram letterboxes it) with the makers caption and
`docs/marketing/youtube/thumbnail-05-cater-with-sweet-hand.png` as the cover.

## Posting

1. Open <https://www.instagram.com/accounts/emailsignup/>, sign up with
   `hello@sweethand.ca`, the name Sweet Hand and the username above, and
   confirm the code Instagram emails.
2. **Edit profile**: upload `page-avatar.png`, paste the bio, add the website
   and set the name.
3. Switch to a professional account (Business, Food & Drink) and add the
   contact email.
4. In Accounts Center, add the Sweet Hand Facebook Page so posts can be shared
   to both.
5. Make the first two posts, then the Reel.
6. Record the account URL here, under a **Published** heading, in the PR that
   follows.
