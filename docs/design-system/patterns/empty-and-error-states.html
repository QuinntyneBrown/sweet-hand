Pattern

Empty and error states

When there is nothing to show, Sweet Hand says why in a line, says what happens next in another, and offers one way forward. Errors own the problem, say what is safe, and give a Try again.

Overview

An empty or failed screen is still a screen people have to act on. Sweet Hand uses two components for them: the empty state (.empty, inside the page shell, in place of the list or region that has nothing) and the error page (.system-page, a whole page for 403, 404, 410, 429, 500, 503 and offline). Both are centred, short, and carry at most two actions: one ink primary and one quiet alternative.

  • Keep the frame. The header, filters and side navigation stay, so people can change course without the Back button. Only the region that has nothing changes.
  • One reason, one next step. The title says what is (or isn't) there; the line under it says why or what will happen; the action moves them on.
  • Food, not illustrations. System pages use the round food photos (.photo-row); in-page empty states use a 24-unit outline icon in a stone circle. No mascots, no cartoons.
  • Errors say what is safe. "Your filters are kept", "Your messages are safe", "Nothing you saved is lost".

Responsive behaviour

  • Empty states are centred in their region at every width; the text is held to 36 characters per line so it stays two or three lines on a phone.
  • Actions wrap and stay centred; on phones the primary comes first and both stay full size (44px).
  • On phones the browse page shows no-results under the "Filters 2" trigger and the active chips, so the way out is one tap away.
  • System pages hold to 34rem; the photo row (72 / 104 / 72px, 280px in all) fits inside 320px without scrolling sideways.
  • In the inbox, a not-found thread replaces only the thread column on large screens; below 62rem it is the whole view with "Back to inbox".

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An empty region is plain content with a heading at the region's level (h2 in a page, h3 inside a section). A load failure that replaces content after the person acted gets role="alert"; a first-run state does not. System pages put the title in the page's only <h1> and return the matching HTTP status (404, 410, 403, 429 with Retry-After, 503).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the primary, then the secondary, then the reference's Copy button.
EnterTry again reloads the region and moves focus to its heading when content arrives.

Focus

After "Clear filters" focus moves to the result count, which announces "60 caterers". After a failed "Try again" focus returns to Try again. Navigating to a system page puts focus at the top as on any page load.

Labelling

Icons are decorative (aria-hidden="true") and photos on system pages have alt=""; the title carries the meaning. Chip remove buttons name the filter ("Remove Haitian").

Content

  • Be specific. "No Haitian caterers list Durham yet" beats "No results". "Tomas Reyes hasn’t removed anything between 3 Oct and 10 Oct 2026" tells an operator exactly what was searched.
  • "Yet" for first runs. It promises things will arrive: "No enquiries yet", "Your first review is on its way", "The first caterers are on their way".
  • Own errors plainly. "Something went wrong on our side." "That one’s on us, not you." Never blame the person or show raw codes as the message.
  • Name a person to ask on forbidden screens: "Ask Leah Mohammed, an Admin, to add or deactivate an operator."
  • Not found never confirms existence. "The link may be old, or it belongs to a different account." The same body is used for missing and not-yours.
  • References are labelled "Reference" and copyable; they go last, in small text.
