Pattern

Feedback and loading

Every action gets an answer in proportion to it: a quiet line for a saved field, a toast for a sent reply, a confirmation inside the dialog for a sent enquiry. Waiting shows the shape of what is coming, and nothing flashes for waits under 300 ms.

Overview

Sweet Hand answers every action, never louder than the action deserves, and close to where the person is looking. The pieces are the inline message and .save-status line, the toast, the alert and banner, the success view inside a dialog, and three ways to show waiting: skeleton, spinner and progress.

  • Feedback lives where the action happened. A field's save shows under the page heading; a sent enquiry is confirmed inside the enquiry dialog; a removed photo is confirmed by a toast with Undo because the tile itself is gone.
  • Wait 300 ms before showing any loading UI. Most requests finish sooner (L2-086), and a flash of grey is worse than nothing.
  • Keep the layout still. Skeletons and placeholders take the final size, so nothing jumps when data lands.
  • Say what is safe. Failures always tell people what was kept: "Your filters are kept", "Your message is still in the box", "Nothing was changed and the caterer wasn’t emailed."

Which message type to use for system news (toast vs banner vs email) is covered in Notifications; empty and failed screens in Empty and error states.

Optimistic or confirmed

  • Optimistic (update first, roll back with a danger toast on failure): reordering photos and dishes ("Photo order saved"), pressing a filter, marking a thread read, tapping Show all 18 dishes.
  • Confirmed (wait for the server): anything that reaches another person or cannot be taken back: sending an enquiry or a reply, sharing contact details, approving, suspending, removing, moderating. A reply shows as a faded "Sending…" bubble at the end of the thread and the box is read-only until the server answers.
  • Undo is offered only for reversible changes a caterer makes to their own page (photo removed, response deleted). Sent messages cannot be unsent, so "Reply sent" has no Undo; an approval is reversed by suspending, so "is published" has none either.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts span the width at the bottom on phones, above the safe area and above the sticky enquiry bar; from 36rem they sit bottom-right at 24rem wide.
  • Skeleton grids use the same column count as the loaded grid at each breakpoint (1 / 2 / 3 columns of caterer cards).
  • The offline banner is full-bleed at every width; its copy is short enough to fit two lines at 320px.
  • The save-status line wraps under the heading on phones rather than truncating.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Status lines (.save-status, .result-count) are role="status" polite live regions present on load, so changes are announced without moving focus (WCAG 4.1.3). Loading regions carry aria-busy="true" and a visually hidden "Loading your enquiries"; skeleton shapes are aria-hidden="true". Uploads use role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow and a name per file. Errors that block an action use role="alert".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches toast actions (Undo, Try again) in the toast region at the end of the page; focusing a toast pauses its timer.
Enter / SpaceActivates Undo, Try again, Cancel upload.

Focus

Feedback never steals focus, with two exceptions that follow the person's own action: a refused submit moves focus to the error summary, and a dialog's success view focuses its first action. After Try again, focus moves to the reloaded region's heading.

Labelling

Busy buttons keep their accessible name ("Send enquiry"); progress is announced in the live region instead. Cancel buttons name the file ("Cancel upload of IMG_2076.heic").

Motion

Under reduced motion the skeleton shimmer and the save-status pulse stop and indeterminate progress becomes a static bar; the spinner keeps turning because it reports status. Toasts appear without sliding.

Touch

Below 48rem every compact control grows to 44px (L2-065.1): toast actions, dismiss buttons, the small Cancel upload button. Compact sizes apply only from 48rem.

Content

  • Progress is status text, not a button label: "Saving…" in the save-status line, "You · Sending…" in a pending message, a hidden "Loading your enquiries" in a busy region. Never "Please wait". (The microcopy library keeps "Saving… · Saved" as the one label that ends in an ellipsis.)
  • Success names the thing and the consequence: "Photo added · “Doubles with tamarind and cucumber chutney” is now on your page." Time-sensitive claims are honest: "live on your page within a minute".
  • Failure says what happened, what was kept, and what to do: "Your reply wasn’t sent · Check your connection, then try again. Your message is still in the box."
  • Slow work is explained, not hidden: "Still processing · IMG_2077.heic is taking longer than usual. It will appear on your page by itself when it’s ready."
  • Offline copy names what still works: "Caterer pages you opened recently still work; enquiries can be sent once you’re back online."
