Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Overlays are for short, focused moments: send an enquiry, confirm a suspension, pick filters on a phone. One at a time, over an inert page, closing back to exactly where people were. Anything long or linkable is a page.

Overview

Sweet Hand has five kinds of overlay, each with its own component: the modal dialog (.dialog + .backdrop), the full-height sheet and drawer (.sheet), the non-modal dropdown and menu (.filter-dropdown, .menu), the popover and tooltip, and the photo viewer. They are the only surfaces that lift off the flat page, so they are the only ones with a shadow.

  • Use an overlay to keep context. The enquiry form opens over the caterer page because the person is still deciding about Pelau House.
  • Use a page when the work is long, has its own URL, or is worth sharing. A profile review, an enquiry thread and the account settings are pages; their edits open dialogs.
  • One overlay at a time. A dialog never opens another dialog. Toasts may appear above a dialog; nothing else does.
  • Phones change the shape, not the content. Small dialogs become bottom sheets below 36rem; the enquiry form fills the screen below 48rem.

Success in the dialog or a toast?

  • In the dialog when the result needs explaining or offers a next step: "Enquiry sent to Pelau House" with what happens next and "Go to your inbox"; "Thanks — we’ll review your profile within 3 business days"; "Your review will appear after a quick check".
  • A toast when the dialog closes onto a page that already shows the result: "Edit details" closes and the account page updates with a "Saved" toast; a signed-out device disappears from the list and a toast confirms.

No stacking

  • A follow-up decision replaces the current dialog's content or becomes its next step. Opening a second modal on top is not allowed.
  • A small confirmation inside a dialog happens in place: "Delete response" turns into "Delete for good?" and the deletion offers Undo in a toast.
  • Toasts sit above every overlay (--z-toast) and never take focus. Tooltips may appear inside a dialog.
  • Opening a dialog closes any open menu, dropdown or popover first.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Modal dialogs, sheets and the viewer follow the APG Dialog (Modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title and aria-describedby the main sentence; the page behind gets inert. The account menu follows Menu Button; filter dropdowns follow Disclosure (aria-expanded on the trigger).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles inside a modal; never reaches the page behind.
EscCloses the overlay (same as Cancel or Close) unless it is busy; non-modal dropdowns and menus close too.
EnterSubmits a form dialog; never submits a destructive action from a reason field.
← →Previous and next photo in the viewer (no wrap-around).

Labelling

Every overlay has a visible title. Close buttons are named ("Close", "Close menu", "Close filters"). Openers carry aria-haspopup="dialog" or "menu".

Motion

Dialogs scale in and sheets slide over --duration-slow (320 ms), the backdrop fades over --duration-base (200 ms), menus and dropdowns scale in over --duration-fast (120 ms). Reduced motion zeroes these durations, so overlays simply appear; the viewer's swipe bounce is removed.

Touch

Close buttons and footer buttons are 44px; bottom sheets put the actions in thumb reach; a swipe in the viewer moves between photos.

Content

  • Task dialogs are titled with the task, in the same words as the button that opened them: "Send an enquiry", "Write a review", "Report this listing". A subtitle names the object: "to Pelau House".
  • Confirmations are titled as a question with the object: "Suspend Pelau House?", "Remove Devon W.’s review permanently?", "Decline Priya’s enquiry?".
  • Consequences come as short sentences or a list, in the order they happen: leaves the listing, email sent, enquiries closed, can it be undone.
  • Buttons: Cancel and a verb that repeats the title's action ("Suspend caterer", "Remove permanently"); success views end with "Done" or the next destination ("Go to your inbox").
