Pattern

Forms

Sweet Hand forms ask for as little as possible, say what each field is for, check on submit, keep everything people typed, and tell them exactly what to fix. Short tasks open in a dialog; long editing lives on its own screen and saves as you go.

Overview

Forms are where Sweet Hand asks people for something: an enquiry to a caterer, a review, a report, a sign-in, a caterer's business details, an operator's reason for suspending a profile. They are built from the form field (label, control, help, error), the controls (text field, textarea, select, checkbox, radio group, file upload), the form layout (.form, .field-row, .form-actions, .form-summary), inline messages and one ink button.

  • No inline forms on pages. A button that starts editing opens a dialog ("Edit details", "Add a dish", "Write a review") or goes to a screen. The exceptions are screens whose whole job is the form: the profile editor sections, sign-in and registration, and the reply box under a thread.
  • One column, few fields. The enquiry form has six fields, and the postal code is optional. Ask only for what the caterer or the platform needs.
  • Check on submit, then help while fixing. Nothing turns red while someone is still typing their first answer.
  • Never lose input. Errors, failures, sign-in detours and timeouts keep every value (drafts last 24 hours).

Layout

  • One column, at most 40rem wide (.form, .dialog__panel--form), fields stacked with --space-5 to --space-6 between them.
  • Pairs only for short, related fields, with .field-row: Event date + Guests, Event area + Postal code, Smallest + Largest guests. The pair becomes two columns from 36rem and stacks below it. Never pair unrelated fields to save height.
  • Order follows the conversation: when, how many, where, why, then the free-text message. The privacy note sits at the end, next to Send.
  • Actions close the form: Cancel then the one primary, right-aligned (.dialog__footer, .form-actions). Autosaving pages have no Save button.

Labels, help and required fields

  • The label sits above the control in --text-label; help sits under the control in --text-body-sm muted; the error sits under the help. Placeholders are examples only ("Name or ID"), never the label.
  • Required is the default and is not marked. Optional fields add <span class="optional">(optional)</span> to the label. No asterisks.
  • Help states the rule before anyone breaks it: "At least 2 days from today", "Pelau House serves 20–300", "20 to 2,000 characters. Shown as Jean-Robert P., event in September 2026."
  • Groups of choices use <fieldset> and <legend> (the rating stars, cuisines, occasions).

Open question 16 (docs/mocks README): L2-038 autosaves during setup, but L2-044 says published edits apply "when they save". The mocks autosave everywhere. Until it is decided, keep autosave and the save-status line on every editor screen, and do not add a Save button beside it; if the decision goes to Save for published profiles, the screen gets .form-actions--sticky with "Save changes" and the status line reports "Unsaved changes".

Validation timing

  1. While typing the first time: nothing. Counters and format hints ("19 / 2,000") update; errors do not appear.
  2. On submit: the client checks every field, renders the summary and the field errors together, and moves focus to the summary. The same rules run on the server; server errors render the same way.
  3. After a field has been touched or submitted: it re-validates on input and on blur, so the error disappears as soon as the value is right and reappears only if it becomes wrong again.
  4. Single-field forms (reply box, decline note) skip the summary: the error sits under the field and focus moves to the field.
  5. Warnings ("This caterer usually serves 20–300 guests") are advisory: they show after blur, never block, and the caterer sees the same warning on the enquiry.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 36rem: .field-row stacks; small dialogs become bottom sheets; footer buttons wrap with the primary last.
  • Below 48rem: the enquiry form fills the screen (.dialog--full) and its footer stays pinned above the keyboard, so Send is always reachable. Long operator forms (Record a breach) become full-height sheets with the title and footer in view.
  • Editor screens keep the save-status line under the heading at every width; on phones the eyebrow "Overview" back link sits above it.
  • Numeric fields use inputmode="numeric" (guests, guest range); postal codes use autocomplete="postal-code" and autocapitalize="characters"; dates use the native picker.
  • Controls are 44px tall at every width and text is at least 16px, so phones do not zoom into a field.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <form> with novalidate (the product's own messages replace browser bubbles), native inputs, <fieldset>/<legend> for groups, and the star rating as a radio group (APG Radio Group). Errors meet WCAG 3.3.1 (identified in text), 3.3.3 (say how to fix), 3.3.7 (no re-entry: values and drafts are kept) and 3.3.8 (password fields allow paste and password managers).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields in visual order, then Cancel, then the primary.
Enter in a text fieldSubmits the form, except in a textarea and in a confirmation's reason field, where it never submits a destructive action.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus into the field it names.
EscCloses a form dialog (same as Cancel) unless it is busy.

Focus

On a refused submit focus moves to the summary (tabindex="-1"), which is announced with its heading and list. Each link targets the control's id. Focus never moves on blur validation. After success in a dialog, focus goes to the first action of the success view; after a page save, focus stays where it was.

Labelling

Every control has a <label for>. Help and error are connected with aria-describedby, error id first. Keep help and error outside the <label> element (use div.field with label.field__label); a label that wraps them makes the help and error part of the field's name.

Errors never rely on colour: each has the ⚠ glyph, text, and a thicker border.

Motion

Errors appear without animation. The busy spinner keeps turning under reduced motion; the save-status pulse stops.

Touch

Controls are 44px; choice rows are at least 24px with the label as part of the target; summary links are in a list with space between them.

Content

  • Labels are short nouns in sentence case: "Event date", "Guests", "Event area", "Your review", "Reason (sent to Kendra)".
  • Field errors say what to do and the rule, in one sentence without "please" or "invalid": "Choose a date at least 2 days from today", "Enter a whole number of guests from 1 to 5,000", "Write at least 50 characters (35 so far)".
  • Summary heading: "Fix N thing(s) to goal": "Fix 4 things to send your enquiry", "Fix 2 things to post your review", "Fix 1 thing to save". The links repeat the field errors word for word.
  • Submit buttons name the outcome: "Send enquiry", "Post review", "Save certificate", "Approve and publish". Cancel is always "Cancel".
  • Privacy notes sit where the data is asked for: "We keep only the first 3 characters", "Pelau House sees your name, not your email or phone."
  • Failure owns the problem and reassures: "Your enquiry wasn’t sent. Something went wrong on our side. Everything you typed is still here. Reference 7F3K-92QD."
