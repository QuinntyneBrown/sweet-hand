Progress and meter

A progress bar shows how far a task has got: a photo uploading, an export being prepared. The setup meter shows how much of a caterer's profile is ready to submit.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="progressbar".progress.meter

Overview

A progress bar is an 8px rounded track with a fill. Sweet Hand uses it in two places:

  • Transfer progress (.progress): one bar per photo while it uploads, under the round preview on the photo tile ("IMG_2076.heic", 64%). It moves on its own and ends when the photo is in.
  • Setup meter (.meter): a label row ("Ready to submit" · "8 of 10 done") above a bar on the caterer dashboard and in the submit-profile dialog. It counts finished checklist items; it moves only when the caterer completes something.

Choose something else when:

  • The wait is short and has no measurable amount (a button saving, a photo loading in the viewer): the button's busy state or a spinner.
  • Content is loading into a known layout (the listing, the inbox): a skeleton.
  • The list of what is left matters more than the amount: the checklist under the meter. The meter summarises it; it never replaces it.
  • A value on a scale that is not progress (a rating, a chart): rating or bar chart.

Fills are ink (--color-action), never pepper red. Progress is not a selection, and a red bar would read as "something is wrong" on a screen where pepper already marks the current section.

Anatomy

  1. LabelWhat is measured, in .meter__label (or a visible caption above a bare .progress). It labels the bar via aria-labelledby.
  2. Value text"8 of 10 done" or "64%": the same words as aria-valuetext. Never colour or length alone.
  3. Fill.progress__bar, width from --value (a percentage), ink, pill ends, eases over --duration-deliberate.
  4. Track.progress: full width, --space-2 (8px) high, --color-bg-subtle-hover, --radius-full.

Variants

Determinate

Set --value on .progress__bar and keep aria-valuenow in step. Upload bars sit under the file name on the photo tile, capped at 8rem wide.

Success and danger are end states that last about a second; the words beside the bar ("uploaded", "didn’t upload") carry the meaning, and a danger end always comes with a way to retry (the tile's Retry, or the dashboard toast "Upload failed · Retry").

Sizes

Progress has one height (8px, --space-2), thick enough to read at a glance on a phone and thin enough to sit under a file name. Width comes from the container: full width in the meter, up to 8rem on a photo tile.

States

Progress is not interactive, so there is no hover, focus, active or disabled. The states are values and outcomes, set with the real ARIA attributes.

Both themes

  • Value changes animate the fill width over --duration-deliberate (500ms); a meter that jumps from 7 to 8 when a section is ticked glides rather than snaps.
  • Cancelled uploads remove the bar and the tile together ("Cancel upload of IMG_2077.heic"); no "cancelled" bar state exists.
  • Paused offline: the bar stops where it is and the caption changes to "Waiting for connection"; it resumes on its own.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar is always 100% of its container; the container decides. The meter spans the main column on every size; upload bars are min(100%, 8rem) under each photo tile.
  • Below 48rem: the dashboard meter sits above the checklist and below the welcome text, full width. The label row wraps if "Ready to submit" and "8 of 10 done" cannot share one line at 320px; it never truncates.
  • From 62rem: the meter stays in .work-main, beside the listing-card preview pane, not in the side navigation.
  • The submit-profile dialog repeats the meter at the top of its body, at the dialog's width (full screen below 48rem).

Theming

The track reads --color-bg-subtle-hover; the fill reads --color-action (ink), --color-success-solid or --color-danger-solid; the meter label reads --color-fg-muted. There are no component tokens; a theme changes the semantic roles.

In dark, ink flips to light stone and the track to a raised dark stone, so the fill still reads as the brighter, filled part. Success and danger use the lighter callaloo and sorrel steps.

/* The fill is one declaration per variant; a theme never edits .progress */
.progress__bar            { background: var(--color-action); }
.progress--success .progress__bar { background: var(--color-success-solid); }
.progress--danger  .progress__bar { background: var(--color-danger-solid); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow and a human aria-valuetext ("8 of 10 done", "64%, uploading"). Indeterminate bars omit the value attributes and set aria-busy="true" on the region they belong to. The setup meter is a progressbar, not role="meter": it measures progress towards a goal, not a reading on a gauge. See WAI-ARIA progressbar; the APG has no separate pattern, so follow its range properties guidance.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the bar; it is not focusable. The controls around it (Cancel upload, the checklist links) are.
Screen reader browse keysRead the label and value text where the bar sits in the document.

Focus

Not focusable and never moves focus. When an upload finishes, focus stays where it was; the outcome is announced by the photo tile's status text or the dashboard toast.

Labelling

Every bar has a name: aria-labelledby pointing at the visible label ("Ready to submit", the file name) or, when there is no visible label, aria-label ("Uploading IMG_2076.heic"). Do not make screen readers announce every percentage: update aria-valuenow freely but announce only milestones (done, failed) through the tile's status text.

Contrast

Meter fill (ink) on track
Meter label on page
Meter label on card
Success fill on card
Danger fill on card

The fill must reach 3:1 against the track (WCAG 1.4.11), because the boundary between them is the information. The checked pairs cover ink on the track and the solid fills on the card; ink (--color-action), success and danger fills against --color-bg-subtle-hover are listed for addition. The track itself does not need 3:1 against the page: the value text carries the amount.

Motion

Fill changes read --duration-deliberate, which reduced motion zeroes, so the bar jumps to the new value. The indeterminate sweep (--duration-loop) stops under reduced motion and the bar becomes a full-width fill at 60% opacity; the caption still says what is happening.

Touch

Nothing to touch. Controls next to a bar (Cancel upload) keep their own 44px target and 8px clearance from the bar.

Content

  • Label what is measured, not the act: "Ready to submit", the file name "IMG_2076.heic". Not "Progress" or "Loading".
  • Value text counts things people recognise: "8 of 10 done" for the checklist, a percentage only for transfers. Use numerals.
  • Name the next step near the meter, not in it: "Start with your description" sits above; the checklist below says what each item needs ("3 to 30, each with a caption and a cover").
  • Outcome words are plain: "uploaded", "didn’t upload", "Waiting for connection". No "Success!".
  • Indeterminate captions say what is happening: "Preparing your metrics export". Never a bare bar.

Drift fixed during extraction: the upload bars set --value on the track while the meters set it on the fill; both work by inheritance, the fill is canonical. The mock kit's default fill was pepper red (--color-accent) and only the meter was ink; every fill is now ink, since pepper means "selected". The fill transition moved from --duration-slow to --duration-deliberate and the indeterminate loop from a literal 1.4s to --duration-loop. The upload bars had a label but no aria-valuetext; it is now required. No mock shows the indeterminate, success or danger bars; they are documented from the kit.
