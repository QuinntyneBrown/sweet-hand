Sticky call to action

The sticky bar keeps "Send an enquiry" under the thumb while people scroll a caterer's photos, menu and reviews on a phone or tablet. From 62rem the enquiry panel in the right column takes over.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stablerole="region".sticky-cta

Overview

The sticky call to action is a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with the caterer's name, their guest range and the page's one ink button. It exists for one job: on the caterer page at XS, SM and MD (below 62rem), the enquiry stays one tap away however far down the menu or reviews someone has read (L2-015.4). At LG and up the same content lives in the sticky .enquiry-panel beside the page, and the bar is hidden.

  • Use it only for the single primary action of a long, read-mostly page.
  • Don't use it for forms (a form's own submit button, or .form-actions--sticky in a form layout), for messages (a toast or banner), or for navigation.
  • Never stack it with another fixed bar at the bottom; the toast region sits above it.

Anatomy

  1. Bar--color-bg-surface with a 1px --color-border-default top rule, no shadow. Padding --space-3 by the page margin, plus the bottom safe area.
  2. Caterer namestrong in --text-label at --font-size-md, one line, truncated with an ellipsis.
  3. Supporting lineThe guest range in --text-body-sm, --color-fg-muted.
  4. Primary buttonMedium ink button, "Send an enquiry", with aria-haspopup="dialog".

Variants

The bar is position: fixed and hidden from 62rem, so the examples use .sticky-cta--static, which keeps it in the flow and visible at every width.

Sizes

The bar has one size: a 44px button plus --space-3 above and below (about 69px) and the device's bottom safe area. The button stays medium; the large 52px version belongs to the desktop enquiry panel.

States

The bar itself has no states; its button does. Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state.

  • Offline: the button is disabled and described by the offline banner at the top of the page (aria-describedby="offline-note"), the same as the desktop panel's button.
  • Loading, error, removed caterer pages have no bar at all: there is nothing to enquire about yet, or any more.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS, SM and MD (below 62rem): fixed to the bottom edge across the full width, at --z-sticky (above content, below dropdowns, dialogs and toasts). The page content gets .has-sticky-cta: bottom padding of --space-24 plus the safe area, so the last review is not hidden under the bar.
  • Safe area: the bar pads its bottom by env(safe-area-inset-bottom), so on phones with a home indicator the button sits above it; pages need viewport-fit=cover in the viewport meta (the mocks have it).
  • LG and up (62rem+): the bar is display: none and .has-sticky-cta drops its padding; the .enquiry-panel in the right column is sticky instead, with a large button and the privacy note.
  • Narrow phones (320px): the name truncates first; the button never shrinks or wraps.
  • On-screen keyboard: the bar has no inputs, and the enquiry form opens as a full-screen dialog, so the keyboard never pushes the bar up over content.

Theming

The bar reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-fg-default (name) and --color-fg-muted (guest line); the button brings its own tokens. In both themes the surface equals the canvas, so the 1px top rule is the only separation: flat, no shadow, in keeping with Sweet Hand's surfaces. In dark the ink button flips to light stone and remains the brightest thing on the screen.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A role="region" landmark labelled "Enquire", so screen-reader users can jump to it; the desktop .enquiry-panel is an <aside> with the same label, and only one of the two is ever displayed. The button follows APG Button and declares aria-haspopup="dialog" because it opens the enquiry dialog.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the button after the page content and before the footer (the bar comes after <main> in the source).
Enter, SpaceOpens the enquiry dialog; closing it returns focus here.

Focus

A fixed bar can cover whatever has focus underneath it (WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured). The page must keep focused elements above the bar: .has-sticky-cta pads the main content, and the system asks for the same padding under the footer and a matching scroll-padding-bottom (CSS change requested), because in the mocks the footer links sit under the bar at the very bottom of the page.

Labelling

The button text is the name. The truncated caterer name is decorative repetition of the page's h1, so its ellipsis loses nothing for screen readers, which read the whole text.

Motion

None: the bar is present from the first paint and does not slide or hide on scroll, so it never moves under a finger about to tap.

Touch

A 44px button in the thumb zone, clear of the home indicator. The bar covers about 69px of the screen, small enough to leave a 360 × 640 phone most of its height.

Content

  • Name, then one supporting fact: the guest range ("Serves 20–300 guests", "Serves 50 guests"). Not the cuisine, rating or price; the page above has those.
  • The button is always "Send an enquiry", the same words as the desktop panel and the dialog title.
  • No marketing copy and no urgency ("Book now!", "Only 2 dates left"). Enquiring is free and commits to nothing.
