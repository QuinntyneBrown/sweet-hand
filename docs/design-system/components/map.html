Map

The operators’ places map: a schematic map of Toronto and the GTA with one lettered pin per place, a card for the selected place, and the same places as an A-to-Z list beside it.

Overview

The map shows the operators' directory of places on a map of Toronto and the GTA. It appears on two screens: the places map, a full-width map beside an A-to-Z list, and the place page and place editor, where a small map shows one place's pin. The directory is for operators only (L2-110), so the map never appears on customer or caterer screens.

  • Use the map to show where several places sit relative to each other, or to confirm one storefront's pin.
  • Use a list for anything people must read or act on in order; the map always has the A-to-Z list beside or below it, with the same rows and the same selection.
  • Don't put a home address, a caterer or a food truck's overnight base on the map. Caterers show an approximate area on their own page; food trucks appear only at the day's stop.

Pins carry a letter for their type (R, T, G, B, F) as well as a colour, so type never rests on colour alone. Pepper marks restaurants because they are the most common type; the selected pin grows and gets a ring rather than changing colour.

The map tiles come from a provider still to be chosen. Until then the land, water, roads and labels are a schematic SVG drawn with surface tokens, which keeps both themes legible.

Anatomy

  1. Map(.map): a focusable region with a hairline border; the canvas scrolls inside it on narrow screens.
  2. Canvas(.map__canvas): fixed 1000 × 640 aspect ratio, at least 40rem wide; pins are positioned in percent of it.
  3. Base map(svg with .map__land, .map__water, .map__road, .map__boundary, .map__label): role="img" with a label naming the area shown.
  4. Controls(.map__controls): zoom in, zoom out and fit all, as icon buttons; they stay in the top corner while the canvas scrolls.
  5. Pin(.pin + type modifier): a 44px button with a teardrop head and a letter.
  6. Cluster(.pin-cluster): a round count for pins that would overlap; its label names the places in it.
  7. Card(.map-card holding a popover): the selected place's type, name, address, status, open-now text and actions.
  8. Legend(.map-legend): one entry per type, plus the draft outline.
  9. Attribution(.map__attribution): the tile provider's credit, bottom right, once a provider is chosen.

Variants

Pin types

  • Storefront pins (.pin--restaurant, --takeout, --grocery, --bakery) use a teardrop head that points at the address.
  • Food truck (.pin--truck) uses a square head: it marks the day's stop, not a fixed address.
  • Draft (.pin--draft, added to a type) is an outline with a coloured letter. It marks a place that is not yet listed.
  • Cluster (.pin-cluster) replaces pins whose heads would overlap at the current zoom; activating it zooms in on them.

Map with list (.map-layout)

The places map pairs the map with an A-to-Z section list (.map-layout__list). Selecting a list row selects the pin, and the reverse; the current row takes aria-current="true".

Small map (.map--sm)

The place page and the place editor show one pin on a canvas that fits its container instead of scrolling. In the editor the pin can be dragged, or moved with the arrow keys, to correct a geocoded address (L2-113).

Sizes

Two sizes: the full map (.map, canvas at least 40rem wide and scrolling inside the frame) and the small map (.map--sm, canvas at 100% of its container). Pins are one size: a 44px target (--target-comfortable) with a 1.75rem head, 1.25rem in the legend.

  • Hover grows the head by 20%. Focus adds the shared focus ring around the head. Selected (aria-pressed="true") grows the head, adds a ring in the pin's colour and raises the pin above its neighbours.
  • Card open: the selected pin's card sits above the pin, or below it when there is no room above inside the frame (the selected mock clips it at the top edge); Escape or the close button closes it and returns focus to the pin.

  • Error: the map or the places failed. An error empty state replaces the map and offers "Use the list".

Responsive behaviour

  • All widths: the canvas keeps its 1000 × 640 ratio and is at least 40rem wide. Below that the map frame scrolls in both directions inside its own border, and the page itself never scrolls sideways. The controls stay in the top-right corner.
  • Below 100rem: .map-layout is one column with the map first and the A-to-Z list below it.
  • 100rem and up: the list is an 18rem column on the left, at most 40rem tall, and scrolls on its own.
  • Small map: always fits its container; it never scrolls.
  • Pins and clusters stay 44px at every width; the card is at most 18rem wide and never wider than the frame minus --space-6.

Theming

Land reads --color-bg-surface-sunken, water --color-bg-subtle, roads --color-border-default and --color-border-strong, labels --color-fg-muted and --color-fg-subtle. Pin colours are semantic: restaurant --color-accent, takeout --color-action, grocery --color-success-solid, bakery --color-warning-solid, food truck --color-info-solid; letters use --color-fg-on-accent. Each pin head has a --color-bg-surface halo so it separates from roads in both themes. A third-party tile layer must offer a light and a dark style, chosen by the same theme attribute.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The map is a role="region" named "Places map" with tabindex="0", so keyboard users can reach it and pan it. The base map is role="img" with a label naming the area. Each pin and cluster is a native <button> using the toggle button pattern (aria-pressed). The card is a non-modal popover (role="dialog", labelled by its heading). The A-to-Z list is the complete text alternative: every pin has a list row with the same name.

Keyboard

Focus

Pins hide the button outline and draw the shared focus ring around the head, which is the visible shape. The ring and the 44px target meet WCAG 2.4.11 and 2.5.8.

Labelling

A pin's label gives name, type, area and any status: "Yard Pot Jerk Centre, restaurant, York · Little Jamaica"; "Coconut Bread Corner, bakery, Etobicoke, draft". A cluster's label gives the count, up to 2 names and "Zoom in to see them". The letter inside the head is aria-hidden.

Motion

The head scales over --duration-fast; with reduced motion the change is instant. Panning and zooming never animate on their own.

Touch

Every pin and cluster is a 44px target. Dragging the canvas pans it; pinch zooms when a tile provider supports it. The editor's pin is dragged with one finger, and the arrow keys are the alternative (WCAG 2.5.7).

Content

  • The summary above the map counts what is shown: "15 places · 1 food truck out today"; filtered, "6 of 15 places · grocery and bakeries".
  • The legend uses the type names from the place editor: Restaurant, Takeout, Grocery store, Bakery, Food truck, and "Draft (outline)".
  • The card's open-now line reads "Open now · until 11 p.m." or "Closed now", in Toronto time.
  • The base map's label names the area: "Map of Toronto and the GTA from Oakville to Oshawa along Lake Ontario".
