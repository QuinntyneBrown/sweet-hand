Avatar

Avatars stand for people: a customer in a caterer's inbox, an operator in the console, you in the header. Caterers are never avatars; they are represented by their food, in a round photo.

Overview

An avatar is a stone circle with a person's initials. Sweet Hand does not ask customers or operators for a profile photo, so initials are the default and the norm. The rule for who gets what is simple:

  • A person — Marcia Thompson (MT), Priya Ramdial (PR), Leah Mohammed (LM) — gets an initials avatar.
  • A caterer — Pelau House, Sugar Dumplin — gets its round cover photo (photo), because the food is the identity. In Marcia's inbox each row shows the caterer's dish; in Kendra's inbox each row shows the customer's initials.
  • A thing that is not a person or a caterer — a report, a held review — gets an icon avatar in the operator queue (flag, star).
  • You get a button avatar at the end of the site header that opens the account menu.

Don't use an avatar to show status (use a status label) or a count (use a count badge), and don't invent avatars for caterers from their initials.

Anatomy

  1. ContentTwo initials (--text-label, scaled per size), a 20px outline icon, or an <img> that fills the circle with object-fit: cover.
  2. Circle--radius-full on --color-bg-subtle; --color-fg-default content; clips anything inside it.

Account menu trigger

button.avatar is the last item in the signed-in header for customers (MT), caterers (KB) and operators (LM). Inside .site-nav it grows to 44px. It has a hover fill and the shared focus ring, and opens the account menu.

Avatar group

.avatar-group overlaps avatars by 8px with a 2px ring in the surface colour. No current screen uses it; it is kept for listing several people on one record, such as the operators who handled a report. Give the group a text equivalent.

Sizes

NJ NJ NJ
Small 32px (--space-8, 12px initials) · medium 40px (--space-10, the default: account menu, operator rows) · large 56px (3.5rem, 18px initials: the account page header). Rows resize their avatar to match their own media: inbox rows 48px, queue rows 56px, header trigger 44px.

States

Static avatars have no states. The account trigger is a button: default, hover (--color-bg-subtle-hover), focus, expanded while the menu is open. Every avatar has a loading skeleton.

  • Expanded has no extra fill today; the open account menu below it is the visible cue, and aria-expanded="true" tells assistive technology.
  • Deleted people: their messages are signed "Former Sweet Hand user" (L2-072). Show an empty circle with no initials beside that label, because initials are personal data and leave with the account. No mock shows this yet.

Responsive behaviour

  • Avatars do not change size by breakpoint; rows set the size they need.
  • Below md (48rem) the customer header drops "Caterers", "Occasions" and "Inbox" into the site menu; for customers the avatar button goes with them (.site-nav__extra), so the account is reached from the site menu. Caterer and operator headers keep the avatar at every width.
  • At 320px the header hides the "Operator" / "for caterers" tag before it would ever squeeze the avatar.

Theming

Avatars read --color-bg-subtle, --color-fg-default, --color-bg-subtle-hover (trigger hover) and --color-bg-surface (group ring). No component tokens. In dark the circle is stone-800 with stone-100 initials; the group ring follows the dark surface so overlapped avatars still separate. Avatars never take a brand colour: pepper would read as "selected", and per-person colours would be a decoration Sweet Hand doesn't use.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Static avatars are decorative: the person's name is always printed beside them, so they get aria-hidden="true" (an image inside gets alt=""). The trigger is a native <button> following the APG Menu Button pattern: aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded. A group with no names beside it is role="img" with an aria-label naming everyone.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the account trigger last in the header. Static avatars are skipped.
Enter, Space, ↓Open the account menu and move focus to its first item.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the avatar.

Focus

The shared ring wraps the circle (2px --color-focus-ring, 3px offset); the offset keeps it clear of the stone fill.

Labelling

The trigger's name says what it opens and whose it is: "Account menu for Leah Mohammed". Initials alone ("LM") are not a name. Initials are the first letter of the first and last name as the person entered them; one letter when there is one name.

Contrast

Initials on the circle
Initials on the hovered trigger
Focus ring on the header

The circle itself is not a boundary anyone needs to find: the trigger is identified by its initials and position, so the stone fill against the canvas is exempt from the 3:1 non-text rule.

Motion

Only the trigger's hover fill transitions (--duration-fast); reduced motion removes it.

Touch

The trigger is 44px inside the header. A button.avatar anywhere else must be given the same 44px (proposed rule); 40px clears the 24px minimum but not the Sweet Hand phone target.

Content

  • Two uppercase initials from the name as entered: Marcia Thompson → MT, Jean-Robert Pierre → JP, Kendra Baptiste → KB.
  • Always print the name next to a static avatar: "Priya Ramdial", "Leah Mohammed · Admin".
  • Icon avatars follow the queue's kinds: flag for reports, star for held reviews.
  • Never put a caterer's business initials in an avatar ("PH" for Pelau House); show the cover photo.
