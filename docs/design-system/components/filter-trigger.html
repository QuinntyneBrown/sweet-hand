Filter trigger

Below 62rem the cuisine column has no room, so filters fold into buttons: a bar of Cuisine and Area dropdowns on tablets, and one Filters button that opens a full-height sheet on phones. Each shows how many filters are on.

Overview

A filter trigger is an outlined pill button that opens filter choices. It is how the filter list travels to smaller screens (L2-009):

  • MD, 48–62rem: a .filter-bar above the grid with one trigger per group, "Cuisine" and "Area". Each opens a .filter-dropdown anchored under it: a short checkbox list, "Clear" and a live count. Choices apply as you tick.
  • XS and SM, below 48rem: a .mobile-browse-bar with one "Filters" trigger and a ghost "Area or postal code" button. Filters opens the full-height sheet (dialogs/filters) with both groups, "Clear" and "Show 60 caterers".
  • LG and up: no triggers. The column is always visible.

When filters are on, the trigger carries an ink count bubble ("Filters 2", "Cuisine 1"). Use a plain button for actions, a menu for a list of commands and a select for one choice inside a form. A filter trigger is only for opening filter choices.

Anatomy

  1. Filters iconPhone trigger only: the sliders icon, aria-hidden="true".
  2. Label"Filters", or the group name ("Cuisine", "Area"), in --text-label at --font-size-md.
  3. Count bubble.filter-trigger__count: ink (--color-action), 22px round, caption semibold. Only when filters are on.
  4. ChevronDropdown triggers only: tells people a panel opens beneath.
  5. ContainerPill, 1px --color-border-strong edge on --color-bg-surface, 44px tall, --space-4 side padding.

Phone Filters sheet

The sheet covers the screen (a side sheet from 48rem), lists both groups as checkboxes with "14 caterers" under each name, and ends with "Clear" and a primary that states the outcome: "Show 60 caterers", then "Show 2 caterers" as you choose.

Sizes

Triggers are 44px at every width (--control-height-md) and only appear below 62rem, where that size is the phone target (L2-065.1). There is no small or large trigger.

States

aria-expanded drives the open look on dropdown triggers; the phone trigger opens a modal, so it uses aria-haspopup="dialog" and has no open look. Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state.

Both themes

  • Open is ink, not pepper: a 1px ink border plus a 1px inset ink shadow make a 2px edge. Opening a panel is not a selection; the count bubble says what is selected.
  • Disabled appears offline (pages/caterers/offline): 50% opacity, with the offline banner saying why.
  • Busy lives in the sheet, not the trigger: "Show 2 caterers" takes aria-busy while results load (dialogs/filters/busy).

  • Below 48rem the dropdown is positioned against the whole bar (.filter-bar becomes the containing block), so a panel opened from "Area" still starts at the left margin and never runs off screen; its width is min(20rem, 100vw − 2 × margin).
  • The browse bar wraps: at 320px "Area or postal code" drops under "Filters" rather than truncating.
  • The sheet respects the top and bottom safe areas in its header and footer padding.

Theming

No component tokens. The trigger reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong and --color-fg-default; hover and press use --color-bg-subtle and --color-bg-subtle-hover; open uses --color-fg-default as a 2px edge. The count bubble is --color-action with --color-fg-on-action, the same ink as the primary button, so in dark it flips to light stone with near-black digits. The dropdown is an overlay, so it is one of the few places with a border and shadow: --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --shadow-3, --radius-lg.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Dropdown triggers are buttons with aria-expanded and aria-controls; the panel is a non-modal role="dialog" labelled by its title, holding a fieldset of checkboxes (APG Disclosure behaviour with dialog semantics, because the panel holds form controls, not commands). The phone trigger has aria-haspopup="dialog" and opens a modal sheet (APG Dialog (Modal)) over an inert page.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, SpaceOn a dropdown trigger: opens the panel and moves focus to the first enabled checkbox. On Filters: opens the sheet and focuses its title.
Tab / Shift+TabDropdown: moves through checkboxes, Clear, then out; leaving the panel closes it. Sheet: focus is trapped until it closes (L2-067.1).
SpaceTicks a checkbox; results and the live count update.
EscCloses the dropdown or sheet and returns focus to the trigger (L2-067.4).

Focus

The shared pepper ring sits outside the pill. When a panel closes, focus returns to the trigger that opened it, whose count bubble now reflects the new state.

Labelling

The count is part of the button's name through visually hidden text: "Filters, 2 active", "Cuisine, 1 selected". Do not put aria-label on the bubble span; a generic element's label is ignored by most screen readers. The sheet title repeats the count the same way.

Motion

The trigger's fill transitions over --duration-fast. The dropdown appears without animation; the sheet slides up (or in from the right from 48rem) over --duration-slow. Reduced motion zeroes both.

Touch

44px tall, 8px apart in the bar (--space-2), which meets L2-065.1. Checkbox rows in the sheet are at least 24px tall with label text widening the target; the whole label is clickable.

Content

  • The phone trigger is "Filters". Dropdown triggers are the group names, "Cuisine" and "Area", never "Filter by cuisine".
  • The bubble is a bare number: how many values are on in that group (or in total for Filters).
  • The sheet's primary states the result: "Show 60 caterers", "Show 2 caterers", "Show 1 caterer". The secondary is "Clear". L2-009.1 names these "Apply" and "Clear"; the mocks chose the outcome wording, which needs the requirement updated.
  • Dropdown footers say "Clear" and the live total ("2 caterers").

Sources

Extracted from these mocks:

  • pages/caterers/default (browse bar and filter bar, nothing on)
  • pages/caterers/filtered and pages/caterers/occasion (count bubbles)
  • pages/caterers/offline (disabled)
  • dialogs/filter-menu/default and dialogs/filter-menu/area (open dropdowns)

Drift fixed during extraction: every count bubble used aria-label on a plain span ("2 active"), which is not reliably announced; the system uses visually hidden text inside the button. The dropdown's scroll limit and footer spacing were inline styles (max-height: min(50dvh, …), margin-top: var(--space-4)); .filter-dropdown fieldset { max-height: min(50dvh, 20rem); overflow: auto; } and .filter-dropdown .cluster { margin-top: var(--space-4); } are proposed. Open: L2-009.1 says "Apply"; the mocks say "Show 2 caterers".
