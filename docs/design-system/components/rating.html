Component · Data display

Rating

Stars, three ways: the caterer's average as a number with one star, a review's whole-star score, and the five-star radio group customers use to rate their event. The number always carries the value; the stars only echo it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.rating.stars.star-input

Overview

  • Summary (.rating + .rating__summary): one filled star and "4.7 · 23 reviews" at the head of a caterer's Reviews section. The average is a number, never a row of partly filled stars.
  • Stars (.stars): five 16px stars showing one review's whole-number score, with role="img" and aria-label="4 out of 5". Unfilled stars use .icon--empty.
  • Star input (.star-input): five native radio buttons in a fieldset ("Your rating") with 44px star labels, used only in dialogs/review.

Use a rating only for customer reviews of a caterer. A count of something ("18 dishes", "2 unread") is plain text or a badge; progress through setup is a meter; the full review with author and response is a review. When a caterer has no reviews, show "No reviews yet" as text, never five empty stars or "0.0".

Anatomy

  1. Summary starOne filled star, decorative (the wrapper is aria-hidden).
  2. Summary text.rating__summary: average to one decimal and the count, --text-h3, tabular figures.
  3. Display stars.stars: 16px filled icons in --color-star, empties in --color-star-empty, 2px apart. One accessible image.
  4. Legend"Your rating" names the radio group.
  5. Filled star labelA 44px round <label> with a 28px star; data-filled paints it --color-star for every star up to the chosen one.
  6. Empty star labelUnfilled stars stay --color-star-empty at 3:1 because they are controls. Each label holds visually hidden "4 stars" text.

Sizes

Two star sizes, chosen by job, not by breakpoint. Display stars are 16px (--icon-size-sm) so they sit on a line of small text; input stars are 28px (--icon-size-xl) inside 44px targets (--target-comfortable) so a thumb can pick one.

States

Display stars are static. The star input has the radio states, shown on the whole group because one star never stands alone. Hover is a subtle disc behind one star plus a preview fill (script sets data-filled on stars 1…n while the pointer is over star n and restores the chosen value on leave).

Responsive behaviour

  • Sizes never change by breakpoint. Five 44px stars plus 8px gaps are 252px, which fits the full-screen review dialog at 320px (288px of content).
  • .stars and .rating are inline-flex and never wrap internally. In a review head the stars drop to their own line when author, date and stars do not fit (the head wraps).
  • In the caterer section head the summary moves under the "Reviews" heading on narrow screens (.caterer-section__head wraps) and sits at the right from about 36rem.

Theming

Two semantic tokens: --color-star (filled) and --color-star-empty (unfilled). Stars are ink, not gold and not pepper: a gold star would add a colour the brand does not have, and pepper means "selected". In dark the filled star is light stone and the empty star a mid stone.

The empty star was raised to 3:1 during extraction. The mocks used --palette-stone-300 in light (about 1.6:1 on the canvas) and --palette-stone-700 in dark; an empty star in the input is part of a control, so it must meet WCAG 1.4.11. It is now --palette-stone-500 / --palette-stone-600. Filled and empty stay distinguishable by shape and by the count text, so their contrast with each other only needs 1.2:1.

To re-skin stars for a campaign, override the two semantic tokens on a theme scope; never colour .stars .icon directly.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Display stars are one role="img" with aria-label="4 out of 5"; the icons inside are aria-hidden. The star input is a native radio group in a <fieldset> with a <legend> (APG Radio Group): the radios are visually hidden but stay in the accessibility tree and take focus; the labels are what you see and tap.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves into the group, to the checked star (or the first star when none is checked), then out to the review text.
→ ↓ / ← ↑Selects the next / previous star and fills up to it; wraps from 5 to 1.
SpaceSelects the focused star when none is checked yet.

Focus

The ring is drawn on the label (input:focus-visible + label) with no offset, following its round shape. In the review dialog the legend is the first thing read, then "1 star, radio, 1 of 5".

Labelling

Each label holds visually hidden "1 star" … "5 stars", so a choice is announced as a number, not as "star". Errors are tied to every radio with aria-describedby, and listed in the dialog's error summary ("Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars"). Summaries outside a heading context get a hidden "Rated " prefix.

Motion

The hover disc fades in over --duration-fast; preview fill is instant. Reduced motion removes the fade. Stars never bounce or sparkle.

Touch

Each input star is a 44px circle with 8px between neighbours below 48rem (4px from 48rem). Display stars are not interactive.

Content

  • Summary format: average to one decimal, a middle dot, the count with the right noun: "4.7 · 23 reviews", "3.0 · 1 review". Always one decimal, even for whole numbers.
  • Accessible label: "4 out of 5". Not "4 stars", not "4/5".
  • Legend: "Your rating". Error: "Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars".
  • Zero state: "No reviews yet" with "Reviews come from customers after their event." Never "0 reviews" or "Be the first!".
