Gallery and viewer

The caterer page opens with their food: a swipeable carousel on phones, a lead photo with thumbnails on larger screens, and a full-screen viewer with captions that works by touch, mouse and keyboard.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.gallery.viewerL2-016, L2-019

Overview

The gallery shows a caterer's photos in the order they chose, cover first (L2-016.1), as round photos. Every photo is a button that opens the viewer at that photo: a full-screen dialog with a "2 of 6" count, previous and next buttons, and the caterer's caption underneath (L2-016.2).

  • XS (below 36rem): a horizontal carousel, one photo at 80% width with the next peeking in, snap scrolling and position dots (L2-019.1).
  • SM and MD: a 24rem lead photo with a strip of up to five thumbnails beneath (L2-019.2).
  • LG and XL: a large lead photo spanning two rows beside a 2 × 2 grid of thumbnails, up to four (L2-019.3). When more photos exist, the last visible thumbnail carries a "+N photos" scrim (.gallery__more).

The gallery is only for a caterer's photos on their public page. Caterers manage photos with photo tiles; the listing uses one cover photo per caterer card. For anything else that needs a full-screen layer, use a dialog or sheet.

Anatomy

  1. Count.viewer__count in the .viewer__bar: "2 of 6", a polite live region.
  2. CloseOutlined 44px icon button, "Close photo viewer", focused on open.
  3. Previous / nextOutlined icon buttons either side of the stage; disabled at the ends (no wrap-around).
  4. Stage photo.viewer__stage .photo: min(100%, 62dvh, 34rem), the 1200w image.
  5. Caption.viewer__caption: the caterer's caption in --text-body-lg, the dialog's description.

On the page, the gallery itself is .gallery (a labelled section) holding .gallery__track (a list), one .gallery__btn per photo, .gallery__dots on phones and .gallery__more on the last visible thumbnail when photos are hidden.

The viewer is the page colour, not a black lightbox: food reads truer on the same canvas it was browsed on, and the flat brand has no dark overlays outside dialogs. (Inside this frame the demo omits aria-modal so it does not hide the docs page.)

Sizes

The gallery fills the caterer page's content column and the viewer fills the screen; their arrangement changes by breakpoint (see Responsive), not by a size modifier.

States

Gallery buttons: hover zooms the photo to 1.08; focus draws a round ring. Hover and focus are forced with data-state.

Viewer states

  • Loading (aria-busy="true" on the viewer): the stage shows a placeholder circle with a large spinner, labelled "Loading photo 3 of 6"; the caption is already there.
  • Failed: a failed circle labelled "Photo 4 of 6 didn't load", the caption, "This photo didn't load." and a small "Try again" button. Arrows stay usable.
  • First and last: Previous is disabled on photo 1, Next on the last. The viewer does not wrap around.
  • Gallery loading: five skeleton circles in the final layout (pages/caterer/loading).

  • Viewer: the stage gap grows from --space-2 to --space-8 at 48rem; the photo is min(100%, 62dvh, 34rem); bar and caption pad for the top and bottom safe areas.
  • The carousel bleeds to the screen edges with --layout-margin padding, so the first photo lines up with the page text and the next one peeks in from the right as a swipe cue.
  • No layout scrolls sideways at 320px apart from the carousel track itself, which is the intended swipe (L2-019.4).

Theming

No component tokens. The viewer is --color-bg-canvas, its count --color-fg-muted, its caption --color-fg-default, its icon buttons outlined in --color-border-default. Dots are --color-border-strong, the current dot --color-fg-default and wider. The "+N" scrim is --color-bg-scrim with --color-fg-on-scrim, the one place text sits on a photo (L2-069.2). In dark the canvas turns near-black, so the viewer becomes dark automatically; photos are never dimmed.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Gallery: a section aria-label="Photos" with a list of buttons, each aria-haspopup="dialog". It is not an auto-rotating carousel; it never moves on its own (APG Carousel guidance on user control applies). Viewer: role="dialog" aria-modal="true", labelled by a visually hidden heading ("Pelau House photos") and described by the caption, over an inert page (APG Dialog (Modal)).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabGallery: one stop per photo, scrolling the carousel to the focused photo. Viewer: cycles Close, Previous, Next (and Try again) only (L2-016.5).
Enter, SpaceOpens the viewer at that photo; focus moves to Close.
← / →Viewer: previous or next photo (L2-016.3). The count updates and is announced.
Home / EndViewer: first or last photo.
EscCloses the viewer and returns focus to the thumbnail of the photo that was showing (L2-016.4).

Focus

Gallery buttons keep --radius-full when focused, so the pepper ring is a circle around the photo. In the viewer the ring sits outside the outlined icon buttons.

Labelling

Each gallery button is named with its position and caption: "Open photo 2 of 6: Curry chicken with rice and buss up shut in a steel karahi". The image inside then takes alt="" so the caption is not read twice. When a "+N" scrim covers the last thumbnail, the name adds "25 more photos in the viewer"; the scrim itself is aria-hidden. Dots are decorative (aria-hidden): position is in the names. Viewer buttons: "Close photo viewer", "Previous photo", "Next photo".

Contrast

Viewer count
Viewer caption
Inactive dot on page
Focus ring on page

The viewer's button outline (--color-border-default) is decorative; the icon in --color-fg-default is the meaningful part and passes at 4.5:1.

Motion

Thumbnails zoom over --duration-slow; the current dot widens over --duration-base; the viewer fades in over --duration-base. With reduced motion, zoom and slide transitions stop and the photo swap is an opacity fade of 200ms or less (L2-069.4); carousel snap scrolling becomes instant.

Touch

Swipe left or right in the viewer to change photo (L2-016.3). Viewer buttons are 44px. Thumbnails are at least 80px on an SM strip and much larger elsewhere.

Content

  • Count: "2 of 6", no "Photo" prefix on screen.
  • Captions are the caterer's own words, 5 to 125 characters, describing the food ("Rum-soaked black cake dusted with icing sugar").
  • More scrim: "+1 photo", "+25 photos"; on small thumbnails "+25".
  • Failure: "This photo didn't load." with "Try again". Never an error code.
  • Order is the caterer's; the cover is always first.

Sources

Extracted from these mocks:

  • pages/caterer/default (six photos, carousel and dots)
  • pages/caterer/edge (failed thumbnail with the caption in its name)
  • pages/caterer/loading (skeleton circles)
  • dialogs/photo-viewer/default, last, busy and failed

Drift fixed during extraction: on the main caterer mock each gallery button's aria-label ("Open photo 2 of 6") overrode the photo's alt, so the caption was never announced, while the edge mock included it; the system names buttons "Open photo N of M: caption" with alt="" inside. .gallery__more exists in the CSS but no mock renders it, so at LG photo 6 of 6 vanished without a cue; the system shows it, and proposes .gallery__more { display: none; } plus @media (min-width: 36rem) and (max-width: 61.99rem) { .gallery__track > li:nth-child(6) .gallery__more { display: grid; } } and @media (min-width: 62rem) { .gallery__track > li:nth-child(5) .gallery__more { display: grid; } } so the scrim sits on the last visible thumbnail at each breakpoint. The failed viewer's "Try again" carried an inline top margin.
