Sheet and drawer

A sheet is a full-height panel for long lists of choices or links: the phone filters and the site menu. It fills the screen on phones and becomes a 28rem side sheet from tablets up.

Overview

A sheet is a modal dialog shaped for content that is tall rather than wide. Sweet Hand uses two, both on phones and small tablets where there is no room for the content on the page itself:

  • Filters (dialogs/filters): the listing's cuisine and area choices, which sit in the left column from 62rem and in popovers at 48–62rem. The footer holds "Clear" and a live "Show 60 caterers".
  • Site menu (dialogs/site-menu): the header links that do not fit below 48rem, as a filter list with the current page in pepper, then who is signed in and "Sign out". Customers, caterers and operators each get their own list.

A drawer (.drawer) is the same panel pinned to the right or left edge at every width. No current screen needs one; it is kept for a future side panel, and .drawer--left for navigation that should slide in from the side it lives on.

Use something else when the content is a decision or a short form (a dialog), a handful of choices that should not cover the page (a popover), or an action list for one object (a menu).

Anatomy

  1. Header.sheet__header: 1px bottom rule, padding --space-3 by the page margin, plus the top safe area.
  2. Title.sheet__title in --text-h4, an <h2> that names the dialog.
  3. Close44px icon button named for the sheet: "Close filters", "Close menu".
  4. Body.sheet__body: scrolls on its own; groups stacked --space-8 apart. A <form> for filters, a <nav> for the menu.
  5. Footer (optional).sheet__footer: 1px top rule, buttons share the width equally, bottom safe area added.

Variants

Live sheets inside a frame. At this window width they show as they would on the same device: full frame below 48rem, a 28rem side sheet over a backdrop above. Live examples leave out aria-modal; the code shows it.

Sizes

One width: the full screen below 48rem and --layout-sheet-width (28rem, 448px) from 48rem. A drawer is 28rem at every width, capped at the screen. Height is always the full viewport; content scrolls inside the body.

The panel's states mirror the dialog's: open, with selections, and busy while results load. Hover and focus belong to the controls inside; the Close button's focus ring is shown below. The small frames set --layout-margin to 16px so the phone padding shows at any window width.

Filters

Filters

  • Selected (dialogs/filters/selected): the title gains the ink count bubble ("Filters 2", announced "2 active"), counts recalculate, options that would give no results become disabled, and the primary reads "Show 2 caterers". Area choices show as removable chips.
  • Busy (dialogs/filters/busy): while the count updates, inputs are disabled, Clear is disabled and the primary is busy with its label kept. Close stays enabled: leaving the sheet just keeps the previous results.
  • Current (site menu): the link for the page you are on uses aria-current="page" in pepper, Sweet Hand's "you are here".

Responsive behaviour

  • XS and SM (below 48rem): the sheet fills the screen and slides up. The header pads by env(safe-area-inset-top) so Close clears the notch; the footer pads by env(safe-area-inset-bottom). No backdrop is visible because nothing shows behind it.
  • MD and up (48rem+): the sheet becomes a 28rem side sheet on the right with --shadow-4 and slides in from the right. Pair it with a .backdrop so the page behind reads as inactive.
  • Where each is used: the filters sheet exists only below 48rem (48–62rem uses filter popovers, 62rem+ the left column). The site menu exists only below 48rem (from 48rem the links fit in the header).
  • Filter choices keep one column at every width; long cuisine names such as "Dominican (Dominican Republic)" wrap under their checkbox.

Theming

Sheets and drawers read --color-bg-surface-raised for the panel, --color-border-default for the header and footer rules, --shadow-4 for the side sheet, and --layout-sheet-width and --layout-margin for size and padding. There are no component tokens.

In light, the raised surface is white like the page, so on phones the header rule is what separates chrome from content. In dark it is stone-850, a step above the canvas, which also lifts the side sheet off the page. The selected count bubble and the primary stay ink (light stone in dark); selected checkboxes are pepper.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A sheet is a modal dialog: APG Dialog (Modal), role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby on the title, page behind inert. The menu's body is a <nav> labelled by the same title, so it is also a navigation landmark. The trigger has aria-haspopup="dialog" and an accessible name ("Menu", "Filters").

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through Close, the body and the footer; wraps inside the sheet.
SpaceToggles the focused checkbox.
EnterFollows a menu link, or submits the filters form ("Show 60 caterers").
EscCloses the sheet without applying unsubmitted filter changes.

Focus

On open, focus goes to Close: the body is long, and starting at the top lets screen-reader users hear the title first. On close it returns to the trigger ("Filters" or the menu button). After applying filters, focus lands on the result count, which is a live region on the listing.

Labelling

Close names the sheet ("Close filters", "Close menu"). The count bubble in the title has aria-label="2 active". Badges in the menu carry their meaning ("1 unread enquiry", "7 items waiting"). Each filter group is a <fieldset> with a <legend>.

Motion

Slide up (phones) or in from the edge (side sheet, drawer) over --duration-slow with --ease-enter. Reduced motion removes the movement; the panel simply appears.

Touch

Close and footer buttons are 44px; the footer buttons split the width so each is a large thumb target. Menu links are 44px rows. Checkbox rows use the whole label as the target and grow to 44px below 48rem (L2-065.1), which is the only range where the filters sheet appears. From 48rem the same rows compact to at least 24px in the mouse-driven filter dropdown and column.

Content

  • Titles are one word: "Filters", "Menu". The count goes in the bubble, not the words.
  • The primary says what will happen with the live number: "Show 60 caterers", "Show 2 caterers", "Show 1 caterer". Never "Apply".
  • "Clear" empties every choice; it does not close the sheet.
  • Option counts use the noun: "14 caterers", "1 caterer", "0 caterers". Cuisine names use the full L2 names ("Trinidadian & Tobagonian", "Barbadian (Bajan)"), never flattened to "Caribbean".
  • The menu says who is signed in, with role where it matters: "Signed in as Leah Mohammed, Admin", "Signed in as Kendra Baptiste, Pelau House".
