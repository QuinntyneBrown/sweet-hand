Caterer card

The listing's core unit: a round food photo, the business name and one quiet line, all one link. Exactly four things, nothing else: no stars, no prices, no badges.

Overview

The caterer listing is a grid of these cards: 60 caterers, 24 a page, in a daily shuffled order (L2-002). Each card shows the cover photo, the business name, the primary cuisine and the primary area, and the whole card links to the caterer page (L2-004). It has no border, no background and no shadow; the photo and the white space around it are the card.

  • Listing (.caterer-grid): the public listing and its filtered and occasion views.
  • Occasion tiles (.occasion-grid): the same card with an occasion name and a count ("Weddings · 23 caterers") on the Occasions page.
  • Preview: "Your listing card" in the dashboard's preview pane, so caterers see exactly what customers see.
  • Draft: on a new caterer's empty dashboard, a non-link card with the "Cover photo" placeholder.

Don't use it for people (use an avatar in a list row), for the operator's caterer table (use table) or for a caterer inside a dialog (use the small photo chip, .dialog__caterer). Never add a rating, price, "New" badge or favourite button: L2-004 forbids them, and they would turn a calm grid into a marketplace.

Anatomy

  1. Cover photoA fluid photo, at most --photo-card-size (280px), --space-5 above the name. Alt is the caterer's caption.
  2. Name.caterer-card__name: --text-card-title (20px medium), ink, clamped to two lines.
  3. Line.caterer-card__meta: "primary cuisine · primary area" in --text-body-sm, muted.
  4. LinkThe whole card is one <a>, centred column, --radius-lg for the focus ring.

Sizes

The card has one size: its width comes from the grid column and its photo is capped at --photo-card-size (280px), so the photo is 280px on a 320px phone (288px column) and still 280px, with more space around it, on a 1440px screen. Type does not change with width.

States

Hover zooms the photo to 1.08 inside its circle and changes nothing else. Hover and focus are forced with data-state.

  • L2-005.4 asks for 4 columns at XL; the design and mocks use 3 beside the filter column at 1440px. This is open question 2 in the mocks README.
  • L2-005.5 asks for a 4:3 photo filling at least 60% of the card; the design uses a circle, which fills well over 60% of the card's height. Open question 1.
  • Names wrap within the column and clamp at two lines on every width; the meta line wraps freely ("Trinidadian & Tobagonian · Whitchurch-Stouffville" takes two lines on a phone).
  • sizes for the photo: (min-width: 62rem) 17.5rem, (min-width: 36rem) 45vw, 90vw.

Theming

No component tokens. The name is --color-fg-default, the line --color-fg-muted, the photo disc --color-bg-photo. There is no card surface, so dark mode only flips text colour and the placeholder disc; the food photo is untouched. Hover deliberately changes no colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A list (<ul class="caterer-grid">) of single links (APG Link), so screen readers announce "list, 24 items". Click, tap or Enter anywhere on the card goes to the caterer page (L2-004.3).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabOne stop per card, in reading order (row by row).
EnterOpens the caterer page.

Focus

The shared ring wraps the whole card, 8px out (--space-2) with --radius-lg corners, so it clears the round photo and the text.

Labelling

The link's name is its content in source order: the photo's caption, then the name, then the line ("Chicken pelau in a steel pot with lime and cucumber chutney, Pelau House, Trinidadian & Tobagonian · Scarborough"). The two-line clamp is visual only; the full name stays in the accessible name (L2-004.4). Occasion tiles have alt="", so they read "Weddings, 23 caterers".

Contrast

Name on page
Cuisine and area line

Motion

The zoom runs over --duration-slow (320ms) with --ease-standard. Reduced motion removes the transition (L2-069.4).

Touch

The whole card is the target: at least 280px tall on a phone. Cards are 48px apart vertically.

Content

  • The name is exactly as the caterer registered it, ampersands and full stops included: "Pimento & Thyme", "Black Cake Co.", "Auntie Merle's Table".
  • The line is "primary cuisine · primary area" with a middle dot and spaces. Cuisine uses the L2-007 name ("Barbadian (Bajan)"), area the L2-010 name ("Downtown Toronto").
  • Never "Caribbean" when the caterer chose a tradition; "Pan-Caribbean" only when it is the caterer's own choice.
  • Occasion tile lines are counts: "23 caterers", "1 caterer".
