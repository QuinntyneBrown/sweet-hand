Component · Data display

Chip

Chips are small pills you can act on: toggle a quick filter, or remove a filter that is already on. A selected chip is pepper red, because pepper means "selected" on Sweet Hand.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.chip.chip--filter

Overview

Chips show choices compactly, in a row that wraps. They are always about filtering or choosing, never about describing a dish.

  • Active filter (.chip--filter): above the caterer results, one chip per filter that is on — "Jamaican", "Scarborough", "Weddings" — each with a remove button, followed by "Clear filters". Operators get the same row on the caterer records list ("“Pelau”", "Suspended").
  • Toggle (button.chip[aria-pressed]): a quick on/off filter where a short row of options beats a list, such as review ratings or "Has photos". Pressed is pepper.
  • Static or link (span.chip, a.chip): a pill that shows a choice already made or goes somewhere. Rare; most read-only pills should be tags.

Not a chip: dietary and allergen labels on dishes are tags (information, not controls). The cuisine and area lists are the filter list and checkboxes in the filters sheet. A choice between views is a segmented control. A record's state is a status label.

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe filter value exactly as in the filter list: "Scarborough", "Trinidadian & Tobagonian". --text-label.
  2. Remove button (filter chips).remove: a circular button with a 16px close icon, named "Remove Scarborough filter". 44px below 48rem, 24px from 48rem.
  3. ContainerPill, 44px tall on phones and tablets below 48rem, 36px (--control-height-sm) from 48rem; 1px border: --color-border-strong at rest, --color-accent when selected.

Variants

Active filters

The row is a labelled group so its purpose is announced once. Chips are always selected-looking (pepper tint), because each one is a filter that is on. "Clear filters" ends the row as a link-style button.

Static and link

Weddings Funerals and repasts Within 3 business days
Link chips go to a filtered listing; a static span.chip only shows a choice. Prefer a tag for read-only facts.

Sizes

Chips have one design size whose height depends on the viewport (L2-065.1): below 48rem every chip is at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable) and the remove button is 44 × 44px; from 48rem chips are compact, 36px (--control-height-sm), with a 24px remove button (--target-min). Side padding is --space-3 at every width, and rows keep --space-2 gaps.

Scarborough

Both themes

Jamaican
Jamaican
  • Selected is the only pepper on the results area, matching the selected item in the filter list beside it.
  • Disabled (50% opacity) is for an option with no results, like the zero-count cuisines in the filter list ("Cuban 0"). Prefer leaving such chips out.
  • Busy: while results reload after a change, the chips stay put and the result count (a polite live region) changes; chips never show spinners.

Responsive behaviour

  • Rows wrap (.active-filters is a wrapping flex row with --space-2 gaps); they never scroll sideways.
  • Below md (48rem) the active-filter row sits under the results heading and above the phone "Filters" trigger's results. The same chips also appear inside the filters sheet's Area group (dialogs/filters/selected).
  • lg (62rem) and up the filter list is beside the results; the chip row stays above the results so the current filters read next to the count.
  • Target size: below 48rem chips grow to 44px tall and remove buttons to 44 × 44px (L2-065.1); from 48rem they are 36px and 24px, for pointer use on wider screens.
  • A very long label ("Religious and cultural celebrations") may make a chip wider than half the phone; it stays on one line and the row wraps around it.

Theming

Chips read --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong and --color-fg-default at rest; --color-bg-subtle / --color-bg-subtle-hover for hover and active; --color-accent-subtle, --color-accent and --color-fg-accent when selected; --color-accent-subtle-hover for the remove button's hover. In dark the selected tint becomes deep pepper (pepper-950) with pepper-300 text and border. No component tokens; a re-skin changes the accent tokens and every "selected" mark moves together.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A toggle chip is a <button aria-pressed> (APG Button, toggle). An active-filter chip is a plain <span> holding a real remove <button>; the chip itself is not focusable. A link chip is an <a>. Rows of chips sit in role="group" with an aria-label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabToggle chips and remove buttons are each one tab stop, in reading order.
Space, EnterToggle a chip, or remove a filter.
After removingFocus moves to the next chip's remove button; after the last one, to "Clear filters", or to the results heading when no filters are left.

Focus

The shared ring goes round the whole toggle chip, or round the small remove button on a filter chip. On the pepper tint the ring keeps 3:1 thanks to its offset onto the page.

Labelling

Toggle chips are named by their label and announce "pressed" / "not pressed". Remove buttons name the filter: "Remove Jamaican filter". A count inside a label ("5 stars 17") should also be in the group's description if it is not obvious.

Selected is not colour alone: aria-pressed is announced, and on screen the filter chip also gains a remove button.

Motion

Background fades over --duration-fast; reduced motion removes it. A removed chip disappears without an exit animation, so focus never lands on something leaving.

Touch

Below 48rem toggle chips are at least 44px tall and remove buttons 44 × 44px, meeting the Sweet Hand phone target (L2-065.1). From 48rem chips are 36px and remove buttons 24px, with 8px gaps, which still clears the WCAG 2.5.8 minimum for pointer users.

Content

  • Use the filter value exactly as the filter list writes it: "Trinidadian & Tobagonian", "Barbadian (Bajan)", "Scarborough", "Weddings". Don't shorten to "Trini" or "Bajan".
  • Searches appear in curly quotes: "“Pelau”". Statuses as written in the segmented control: "Suspended".
  • Remove buttons: "Remove <value> filter"; for a search, "Remove search “Pelau”".
  • The row always ends with "Clear filters" when one or more chips are on.
