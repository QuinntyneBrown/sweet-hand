Popover

A popover is a small raised panel anchored to the control that opened it. It does not block the page: Sweet Hand's filter dropdowns and account menu are popovers.

Overview

Use a popover for a few related controls or a short explanation that belongs to one trigger and should leave the page usable: ticking a cuisine updates the results behind the dropdown within a second. Three shapes share the same surface and behaviour:

  • .popover: the generic panel, with an optional .popover__title and .popover__actions. Up to 20rem wide, sized to its content. No current screen uses it; it is kept for short help next to a control (illustrated below with a metric definition).
  • .filter-dropdown: the listing filters on tablets (48–62rem), opened by a filter trigger ("Cuisine", "Area"). A fixed 20rem panel with a filter group, Clear and the live count.
  • .account-menu: opened by the avatar in the header. It shows who is signed in, then a menu of Inbox, Account and Sign out.

Use something else when:

  • The person must decide before carrying on, or the content is a form: a dialog.
  • The list is long or the screen is a phone: a sheet (the same filters become a full-screen sheet below 48rem).
  • It is a plain list of actions for one object: a menu.
  • It is a one-line name for an icon: a tooltip, or better, a visible label.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-expanded and aria-controls, wrapped with the panel in .menu-anchor so the panel can be placed under it.
  2. Title (optional).popover__title: --text-label at --font-size-md; labels the panel.
  3. ContentOne or two sentences, or one filter group. Muted body text.
  4. Actions (optional).popover__actions: small buttons, right-aligned, --space-2 apart.
  5. Panel--color-bg-surface-raised, 1px --color-border-default, --radius-md (--radius-lg for the dropdown and account menu), --shadow-3, --z-popover.

Area

Area

  • Open: the panel scales in from its anchor corner (top left for dropdowns, top right for the account menu).
  • With selection: ticked boxes are pepper; the trigger shows the ink count bubble and keeps it after the dropdown closes.
  • Updating: the live count in the panel ("14 caterers") is a role="status" region; there is no busy overlay because the panel never blocks.
  • Disabled options (0 caterers) stay in place so the list does not jump.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS and SM (below 48rem): no filter dropdowns; the "Filters" button opens the sheet. When a popover is used this narrow, the filter bar becomes the positioning parent, so the panel starts at the bar's left edge instead of under a trigger near the right that would push it off screen; widths never exceed the screen less the page margins.
  • MD (48–62rem): filter dropdowns open under their trigger in the filter bar.
  • LG and up (62rem+): filters live in the left column as a filter list; no dropdowns.
  • Account menu: from 48rem, under the avatar. Below 48rem the avatar is hidden with the other header extras and the site menu sheet lists the same links.

Theming

All three read --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --shadow-3 and --radius-md/--radius-lg; they stack at --z-popover (generic) or --z-dropdown (filter dropdown, account menu), both below dialogs. In dark, the raised surface steps up to stone-850 and the shadow deepens, so the panel still separates from the stone-950 page. Never tint a popover; selection colour belongs to the controls inside it.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A popover is a non-modal dialog: role="dialog" without aria-modal, labelled by its title, opened by a button that follows the APG Disclosure pattern (aria-expanded, aria-controls). The account menu's list follows APG Menu Button inside the panel. The native popover attribute with popovertarget gives top-layer rendering and light dismiss for free and is the preferred implementation.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter, Space on the triggerOpens or closes the panel.
TabMoves into the panel's controls, then on to the next control on the page, which closes the panel. Focus is never trapped.
↓ / ↑Account menu only: move between menu items; Home/End jump to first and last.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.

Focus

Focus moves in only when the panel has controls: the Area dropdown focuses its search field, the Cuisine dropdown its first checkbox, the account menu its first item. A text-only popover leaves focus on the trigger. Clicking outside closes without moving focus.

Labelling

The trigger names the panel ("Cuisine", "Account menu for Marcia Thompson"); its count bubble says "1 selected". The panel is labelled by its heading. The avatar trigger should say aria-haspopup="dialog", matching what opens.

Motion

scale-in over --duration-fast (120ms) with --ease-enter; closing is instant. Reduced motion zeroes the duration.

Touch

Filter triggers are 44px pills; account menu items are 44px rows; the avatar is 40px with the header's spacing around it. Tapping outside closes the panel and keeps whatever was ticked.

Content

  • Popover titles are short statements, not questions: "How this is counted", "Figures lag by up to 60 minutes".
  • Two sentences at most. If you need a list or a link to more, use a page.
  • Filter dropdowns reuse the filter vocabulary exactly: full cuisine names, counts as "14 caterers", Clear, and the result count instead of an Apply button.
  • The account menu shows the person's name and email first, so a shared device makes it obvious who is signed in.
