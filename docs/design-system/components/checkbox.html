Checkbox

Checkboxes let people pick any number of options: the islands a caterer cooks from, the areas they serve, the dietary tags on a dish. A single checkbox records one agreement, like the privacy notice.

Overview

A checkbox is a native <input type="checkbox"> wrapped in a <label class="choice">, so the whole row (box, label and description) is one click target. Each box is independent: ticking Guyanese does not untick Trinidadian & Tobagonian.

  • Use it for "pick any" lists that are short enough to show in full (18 cuisines, 8 occasions, 8 dietary tags, the areas in one region), and for one agreement that must be ticked before submitting ("I agree to the privacy notice", "I understand this review can't be restored").
  • Use a radio group when exactly one option must be chosen (primary cuisine, report reason, operator role).
  • Use a switch only for a setting that takes effect the moment it changes. A checkbox inside a form waits for the form to save (or autosave).
  • Use the filter list for the listing's cuisine and area filters on large screens; the same choices become checkboxes inside the phone filter sheet (dialogs/filters) and the tablet filter dropdowns.
  • Use a combobox when the list is too long to scan, such as postal codes.

The checked box fills with pepper red (--color-accent). That is the brand's one meaning for pepper: "selected". Never colour a checkbox ink or green to make it look like an action or a success.

Anatomy

  1. Box20px square (--icon-size-md), --radius-xs corners, 1px --color-border-strong. Checked: pepper fill with a clipped white tick; indeterminate: a 2px bar.
  2. LabelBody text in .choice__text. It is the accessible name; keep it to the option itself ("Halal", "Scarborough").
  3. Description (optional)A <small> inside .choice__text, in --color-fg-muted: a consequence or a count ("14 caterers").
  4. Hit areaThe whole <label class="choice"> row, --space-3 between box and text; clicking anywhere toggles.

Variants

Single, with a consent summary

A required agreement is one checkbox whose label links to the full text. .consent-summary sits under it, indented to the label, and says in plain words what the person agrees to. It is tied to the box with aria-describedby.

We use your name and email to run your account and pass your enquiries to caterers. Caterers see your name, never your email or phone unless you share them. Everything is stored in Canada.

From pages/register. The link inside the label is its own focus stop; clicking the label text outside the link toggles the box.
HTML
<div class="field">
  <label class="choice"><input type="checkbox" id="consent" name="consent" required aria-describedby="consent-summary">
    <span class="choice__text"><span>I agree to the <a href="/privacy">privacy notice</a></span></span></label>
  <p class="consent-summary" id="consent-summary">We use your name and email to run your account…</p>
</div>

With a description or a count

Description from dialogs/photo-details; counts from the phone filter sheet, where a zero-count cuisine is disabled rather than hidden so the list never jumps.
HTML
<label class="choice"><input type="checkbox" name="cover">
  <span class="choice__text">Use as cover photo<small>Shown on your listing card and when your page is shared</small></span></label>
<label class="choice"><input type="checkbox" name="cuisine" value="Bahamian" disabled>
  <span class="choice__text">Bahamian <small>0 caterers</small></span></label>

Group in a fieldset, laid out with .option-grid

Related boxes share a <fieldset> whose <legend> is the question. .option-grid puts them in one column on phones and two from 36rem. Dietary and allergen tags are structured data, so they are always checkboxes, never free text in the description.

Nested: a region with its areas

In pages/profile-cuisines each region has an "All of …" parent above its areas. The parent is checked when every area is, unchecked when none is, and indeterminate (mixed) when some are. Ticking the parent ticks every area; unticking it clears them.

Durham
Pelau House serves Ajax and Pickering, so "All of Durham" is mixed. In the product set input.indeterminate = true from script; data-state="indeterminate" only draws it here.
HTML
<fieldset class="stack stack--sm">
  <legend>Durham</legend>
  <label class="choice"><input type="checkbox" name="region" value="Durham" aria-describedby="durham-count">
    <span class="choice__text">All of Durham<small id="durham-count">2 of 8 areas</small></span></label>
  <div class="option-grid" style="padding-left: var(--space-8);"> …areas… </div>
</fieldset>
<script>regionBox.indeterminate = checkedAreas > 0 && checkedAreas < totalAreas;</script>

Sizes

Checkboxes have one size: a 20px box beside body text. The row, not the box, is the touch target, so there is no reason for a larger box; density comes from .option-grid columns, never from a smaller box.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; checked, disabled and invalid use the real checked, disabled and aria-invalid attributes. Indeterminate is a DOM property (indeterminate) with no attribute, so docs draw it with data-state="indeterminate".

Both themes

Group error

When a rule applies to the whole group ("Choose between 1 and 5 cuisines"), the error goes under the legend and help, the fieldset lists it in aria-describedby, and only the boxes that break the rule get aria-invalid="true" (in pages/profile-cuisines/invalid, the six ticked cuisines). The form summary links to the first offending box.

Cuisines

Choose 1 to 5 islands or traditions you cook from.

Choose between 1 and 5 cuisines

Six ticked, five allowed. The error names the rule, not the person's mistake.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 36rem .option-grid is one column; long labels such as "Dominican (Dominican Republic)" or "Religious and cultural celebrations" wrap under themselves, never under the box.
  • From 36rem the grid is two equal columns with --space-6 between them. It never goes to three: in dialogs and the 40rem form column, three columns would truncate cuisine names.
  • Phones (XS/SM): L2-065 asks for 44px targets with 8px between them. A .choice row is about 32px today, so components.css needs a phone rule that raises the row to --target-comfortable and the grid row gap to --space-2 (listed in the extraction report). Until then, do not pack checkboxes tighter than the option grid.
  • Filters: from 62rem the listing uses the filter list; at 48–62rem the same options are checkboxes in a filter dropdown; below 48rem they are checkboxes in the full-screen filter sheet.
  • Nested areas keep their --space-8 indent at every width; the indent is the only thing that shows they belong to the region.

Theming

The checkbox reads semantic tokens directly; it has no component tokens. The box is --color-bg-surface with a --color-border-strong edge; checked and indeterminate fill with --color-accent and draw the mark in --color-fg-on-accent; invalid adds a 1px --color-danger-solid ring outside the border.

In dark the box takes the near-black surface (--palette-stone-950), the border stays --palette-stone-500 (above 3:1 on both themes), and pepper lifts to --palette-pepper-300 with a near-black tick, so the checked box stays the brightest thing in the row. Re-skin by overriding --color-accent on a wrapper, never by restyling .choice input; the same token drives the selected filter, the current tab and the focus ring, which must stay in step.

/* Forced colours: the box keeps a system border and the tick uses the system highlight. */
@media (forced-colors: active) { .choice input:checked { background: Highlight; border-color: Highlight; } }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native checkbox (APG Checkbox). appearance: none only restyles it; the role, the checked and mixed states, form submission and required all stay native. Groups are a <fieldset> with a <legend>, which screen readers announce on entering the group ("Cuisines, group").

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the next or previous checkbox. Every box is its own tab stop (unlike radios).
SpaceToggles. On an indeterminate parent, Space checks it and all its children.
EnterDoes not toggle; inside a form it submits, which is why autosaved profile forms have no implicit submit.

Focus

The shared ring (2px --color-focus-ring, 3px offset) draws around the box only, not the whole row, so it does not collide with neighbouring rows in the grid. Focus is never moved programmatically to a checkbox except from an error-summary link.

Labelling

  • The wrapping label gives the name; no for/id is needed unless the error summary links to the box, which then needs an id (#c-grenadian).
  • Descriptions in <small> are part of the label, so they are read with the name. Use aria-describedby for anything outside the label: the consent summary, the group help, the error.
  • A label with a link ("I agree to the privacy notice") keeps the link short and the rest of the label clickable.

Disabled boxes and labels (--color-fg-disabled) are exempt from contrast minimums (WCAG 1.4.3); they are only used for zero-count filter options, where the "0 caterers" description explains why.

Motion

Background and border colours transition over --duration-fast; the tick appears without animation. Reduced motion zeroes the duration.

Touch

The full label row is the target, so a tap on "Trinidadian & Tobagonian" anywhere toggles it. See Responsive for the 44px phone row height that components.css still needs to meet L2-065 in full.

Content

  • Labels are the option, in the form people use: cuisine names exactly as L2-007 lists them ("Trinidadian & Tobagonian", "Barbadian (Bajan)", "St. Lucian"), area names from L2-010 ("East York", "Whitchurch-Stouffville").
  • The legend asks the question or names the set: "Cuisines", "Occasions", "Dietary tags", "Information involved". The help line says the rule: "Choose 1 to 5 islands or traditions you cook from."
  • An agreement label starts with "I": "I agree to the privacy notice", "I understand this review can't be restored", "I sell Caribbean food in Toronto or the GTA".
  • Descriptions are one short line: a consequence ("Shown on your listing card…") or a count ("14 caterers", "1 caterer", "0 caterers"; singular for one).
  • Errors state what to do: "Tick the box to agree to the privacy notice", "Choose between 1 and 5 cuisines". Never "Required" or "Invalid".
  • Parents read "All of Durham", not "Select all".
