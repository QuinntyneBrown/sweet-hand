Dialog

A dialog stops the page for one decision or one short form: send an enquiry, suspend a caterer, remove a photo. On phones it rises from the bottom; the enquiry form fills the screen.

Overview

Use a dialog when the person must finish or abandon one focused task before carrying on, and the task is short enough to fit on one panel: a confirmation, a form of up to about six fields, or the result of either. The page behind becomes inert and is dimmed by a backdrop.

  • Default: a confirmation that changes something but is easy to undo. "Approve Auntie Bev's Kitchen?", "Sign out of Safari on iPhone?". The primary is ink.
  • Destructive: removes, suspends or deletes. A danger icon in the header and a danger button that names the loss: "Suspend caterer", "Remove photo", "Delete my account".
  • Form: collects input. Wider panel (.dialog__panel--form), fields in the body, a submit button in the footer: "Send an enquiry", "Add a dish", "Report this review".
  • Success: confirms a task that has a follow-up worth reading. A success icon, one or two sentences and a single "Done" or a next step ("Go to your inbox").

Use something else when:

  • The panel is a long list of choices or links, such as phone filters or the site menu: use a sheet.
  • The choice is small and should not block the page, such as the cuisine filter on tablets or the account menu: use a popover.
  • Nothing needs deciding, only confirming that something happened ("Photo removed. Undo"): use a toast.
  • The task has steps, sections or more than about six fields, such as registering as a caterer or setting up a profile: use a page with a form layout.

Anatomy

  1. Backdrop.backdrop: --color-bg-backdrop over the inert page, at --z-overlay. Clicking it does nothing; Escape and Cancel close.
  2. Header icon (optional).dialog__icon--danger|info|success|warning: a 40px tinted circle. Decorative; the title says the same thing.
  3. Title.dialog__title in --text-h3, an <h2> referenced by aria-labelledby.
  4. DescriptionOne sentence of consequence in .text-muted, referenced by aria-describedby.
  5. CloseA 44px icon button named "Close". Same as Cancel.
  6. Body.dialog__body: fields, checklists, alerts, photos. Padding --space-4 --space-6; scrolls when tall.
  7. Footer.dialog__footer: right-aligned, wraps, sticky to the panel bottom. Cancel first, primary last.
  8. Panel.dialog__panel: --color-bg-surface-raised, --radius-lg (--radius-xl top corners on phones), --shadow-4, 32rem by default.

  • Invalid: on submit, an error summary (.form-summary, role="alert", focused) lists each problem as a link to its field; each field gets aria-invalid="true" and a .field__error joined by aria-describedby. The dialog never closes on invalid input.
  • Busy: aria-busy="true" on the panel; fields become read-only (not disabled, so their values stay legible); Cancel and Close are disabled together and Escape is ignored; the primary shows its spinner and keeps its label; a visually hidden role="status" line says what is happening ("Suspending Pelau House").
  • Failed: an .alert--danger with role="alert" at the top of the body says what failed, that nothing changed and that input is kept. Buttons return to normal for a retry.
  • Success: either the dialog closes and a toast confirms (most actions), or the panel swaps to the success layout in place, keeping the title as the label (enquiry, report, review, export, submit profile).
  • Rarer mock states reuse these: conflict (decline-enquiry/conflict: someone replied first) and blocked (remove-photo/blocked: keep at least 3) are warning-toned default dialogs; duplicate and limit in the enquiry and report forms are failed states with their own copy.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS (below 36rem): every dialog is a bottom sheet. The panel spans the width, sits on the bottom edge with --radius-xl top corners only, slides up, and pads its bottom by env(safe-area-inset-bottom) so the footer clears the home indicator. Footer buttons wrap; the primary stays last.
  • SM and up (36rem+): the panel is centred with --space-6 around it, rounded on all corners (--radius-lg), and scales in.
  • Enquiry below 48rem: .dialog--full makes the panel fill the screen (100dvh, no radius) because the form is long and typed on a phone keyboard; the sticky footer keeps "Send enquiry" above the keyboard (L2-026.1). From 48rem it is a normal 40rem form dialog.
  • Height: the panel never exceeds the viewport less 48px (100dvh, so mobile browser bars are respected). Long bodies scroll inside the panel; header and footer stay put.

Theming

The dialog reads semantic tokens directly: --color-bg-surface-raised for the panel and the sticky footer, --color-bg-backdrop, --shadow-4, --radius-lg/--radius-xl, and the status -bg/-icon pairs for header icons. Width comes from --layout-dialog-width; the other widths are fixed rems in the component.

This is the one place Sweet Hand lifts a surface: the panel has a shadow because it floats over the page. In dark, the raised surface is stone-850, a step lighter than the stone-950 canvas, and the backdrop deepens from 50% to 66% black so the panel separates without a border. Icon tints switch to their 950-step backgrounds with 300-step glyphs. Re-skin by overriding those semantic tokens on a theme scope; never give a dialog a pepper header or a coloured panel.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Dialog (Modal): role="dialog" and aria-modal="true" on .dialog, aria-labelledby on the title, aria-describedby on the consequence sentence. Everything outside gets inert, so focus, find-in-page and screen-reader browsing cannot reach it. Use the native <dialog> element with showModal(), or the UI framework's accessible dialog primitive, to get this behaviour; don't hand-roll it. Destructive confirmations stay role="dialog": the person asked for them. role="alertdialog" is reserved for interruptions the system starts.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the panel's controls and wraps from the last to the first; focus never leaves the dialog.
EscCloses, same as Cancel and Close. Ignored while busy.
EnterIn a field, submits the form (the footer primary is type="submit"). In a textarea it adds a line.
Space, Enter on a buttonActivates it.

Focus

  • On open: the first field in a form; Cancel in a destructive dialog without fields; the primary in a default confirmation; Done or the next step in a success panel.
  • On close: back to the button that opened it. When that button is gone (the photo was removed), to the next item in the list, or the list heading.
  • On invalid submit: to the error summary, which links to each field.
  • The sticky footer must not cover a focused field (WCAG 2.4.11): the body scrolls the field into view above it.

Labelling

Close is an icon button named "Close" (the dialog's name gives context; "Close filters" and "Close menu" are used on sheets). Header icons are aria-hidden. Photos in the header and in .dialog-people have empty alt because the names beside them say the same; .dialog-people itself is a list with an aria-label ("Caterers using Pan-Caribbean").

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the panel scales in (SM+) or slides up (XS) over --duration-slow (320ms) with --ease-enter. Closing is instant. Reduced motion removes both animations; the busy spinner keeps turning because it reports status.

Touch

Close and every footer button are at least 44px. The bottom sheet keeps the actions in the thumb zone on phones, and the safe-area padding keeps them clear of the home indicator. Tapping the backdrop does not close a dialog, so a stray tap never loses a typed enquiry.

Content

  • Titles: confirmations are questions that name the thing: "Suspend Pelau House?", "Remove this photo?", "Sign out of Safari on iPhone?". Forms are the task as a verb phrase: "Send an enquiry", "Add a dish". Success is a statement: "Export requested", "Enquiry sent to Pelau House". Never "Are you sure?" or "Confirm".
  • Description: one sentence of consequence, in plain words: "It leaves the listing within a minute and its page returns 'not found'. You can reinstate it later."
  • Buttons: the primary repeats the title's verb ("Suspend caterer", "Retire cuisine", "Approve and publish"). The other is always "Cancel"; a success panel's only button is "Done" unless there is a next step.
  • Failure copy: what failed, that nothing changed, what is kept: "We couldn't suspend Pelau House. Nothing has changed. Check your connection and try again; your reason is kept."
  • Field help in dialogs names the caterer and the limit: "Pelau House serves 20–300", "We keep only the first 3 characters".

  • 10 busy mocks (approve-profile, change-password, decline-enquiry, delete-account, download-data, edit-details, end-session, request-changes, review-response, search) disabled Cancel but left Close enabled. Both are disabled while busy.
