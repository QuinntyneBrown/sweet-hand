Message thread

The enquiry conversation between a customer and a caterer: who it is with and where it stands, the messages, and a reply box pinned to the bottom. It is the only place on Sweet Hand where contact details are ever exchanged, and only when someone chooses to share them.

Overview

A thread is one enquiry: a customer asked a caterer about an event, and the two of them talk it through. The same component serves both sides. The customer reads it at /inbox/:id (pages/thread); the caterer reads it in the dashboard (pages/dashboard-enquiry). "Mine" and "theirs" flip with the viewer; the markup does not.

  • Thread head (.thread__head) names the other party with their round photo (customer view) or initials (caterer view), the enquiry status and the thread actions: "Share my contact details", "Close enquiry", "Decline".
  • Messages (.messages, an ordered list) hold three kinds of item: theirs (light bubble, left), mine (ink bubble, right) and system events (centred text: declined, closed, contact shared).
  • Reply box (.reply-box) is a labelled textarea with a send button, sticky to the bottom of the viewport. When nobody can reply any more it is replaced by a closed note (.closed-note).
  • Placeholder (.thread-placeholder) fills the thread column on large screens before an enquiry is chosen.

Use it only for enquiry conversations. A caterer's public answer to a review is a review response, not a message. A history of who did what (operator actions, profile changes) is a timeline. A one-off confirmation such as "Enquiry sent" is a toast. The list of threads is made of inbox rows.

Anatomy

  1. Party photo or initials3.5rem round photo (customer view) or avatar with initials (caterer view). Decorative: alt="", the name follows.
  2. Title and status line.thread__title holds the heading (the page's h1, styled --text-h3) and .thread__status: a status label plus "View caterer" or "Sent yesterday at 4:15 p.m.".
  3. Their bubble.message--theirs: --color-bg-subtle, --radius-lg with a squared bottom-left corner pointing at the sender.
  4. Meta.message__meta: sender and time in --text-caption, --color-fg-subtle. Carries "Sending…" and "Not sent" too.
  5. My bubble.message--mine: ink (--color-action) with --color-fg-on-action text, right-aligned, squared bottom-right corner.
  6. Reply label and field.reply-box__label + a textarea at --control-height-lg with --radius-lg. Help and counter sit underneath.
  7. SendThe screen's one ink primary. Icon-only "Send reply" on the customer side; "Send" with an icon on the caterer side.

Sizes

The thread has one size. Bubbles are capped at min(36rem, 88%) of the column so lines stay readable; density never changes with the viewport.

Responsive behaviour

  • Below 62rem the inbox shows the list or the thread, never both (.inbox-layout--thread hides the list). A "Back to inbox" eyebrow (.until-lg) sits above the head.
  • 62rem and up the list (20–26rem) sits beside the thread; with nothing selected the thread column shows .thread-placeholder. The caterer's copy lives in the dashboard .work-layout, beside the side navigation.
  • Bubbles take at most min(36rem, 88%); at 360px that is about 290px, so "mine" and "theirs" still read as two sides. Text uses white-space: pre-wrap (keeps the sender's line breaks) and overflow-wrap: anywhere so a pasted URL wraps at 320px (thread/edge).
  • The head wraps: on phones the actions drop below the title, and the small action buttons grow to 44px below 48rem (L2-065.1); they return to 36px from 48rem. The reply box is position: sticky; bottom: 0 with env(safe-area-inset-bottom) padding, so the send button clears the home indicator; the textarea grows, the 44px send button stays beside it.
  • No breakpoint changes the order of messages or hides any of them; a long thread (thread/long) simply scrolls.

Theming

The thread has no component tokens; it reads semantic tokens directly. "Mine" deliberately reuses the action pair (--color-action / --color-fg-on-action) so your own words read as yours, in ink, like the wordmark. Pepper red never appears in a bubble: it would read as "selected".

  • Their bubble: --color-bg-subtle with --color-fg-default. Meta: --color-fg-subtle. Failed meta: --color-danger-fg.
  • Closed note: --color-bg-surface-sunken with --color-fg-muted. Reply box: --color-bg-canvas with a --color-border-default top rule so text never shows through when it is stuck.
  • In dark, the ink bubble flips to light stone with near-black text (as the primary button does) and their bubble becomes a lifted dark stone; both keep at least 4.5:1.

To re-skin, override the bubble on a theme scope, never per message: [data-theme="x"] .message--mine .message__bubble { background: var(--color-action); }.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An <article aria-labelledby> named by the party's name; messages are an <ol aria-label="Messages"> because order matters. This follows the spirit of the APG Feed pattern without its keyboard model: the list is short and static, so plain reading order is enough. The reply is a native <form>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBack to inbox → View caterer → thread actions → any link or "Try again" in the messages → reply field → Send.
EnterIn the reply field, a new line (messages are multi-line). It never sends.
Ctrl/⌘+EnterSends from the reply field, same as the Send button.
Enter, SpaceOn Send or "Try again", sends.

Focus

After sending, focus stays in the (now empty) reply field. After an invalid send, focus moves to the error summary (tabindex="-1"), whose link returns to the field. Opening a thread from the inbox moves focus to the thread h1.

Labelling

  • Each message starts with a visually hidden sender ("Pelau House:", "You:") so a screen reader never relies on bubble side or colour. The meta line repeats the sender for sighted users.
  • The reply field has a visible label ("Your reply", "Reply to Priya") and aria-describedby pointing at the help ("Pelau House gets an email. Links show as plain text."), the counter, and the error when there is one.
  • Icon-only send is aria-label="Send reply". "Try again" on a failed message is a real button.
  • "Sending…" lives in a role="status" meta so it is announced once; a failure is announced by the danger toast (role="alert", notifications/toast/danger).

A pending bubble sits at 60% opacity; its label and the "Sending…" text carry the state, and the composite stays above 4.5:1 in both themes (about 4.8:1 in light).

Motion

New messages appear in place without sliding. The send button's busy spinner turns (it reports status) and keeps turning under reduced motion. Scrolling to the newest message on open uses scroll-behavior, which reduced motion sets to auto.

Touch

Send is 44px. Thread actions are small buttons: 44px tall below 48rem (L2-065.1), 36px with 8px between them from 48rem. "View caterer" and "Back to inbox" (an eyebrow at 44px tall) are text links with comfortable line height. Text in bubbles is selectable, so a customer can copy an address the caterer shared.

Content

  • Meta is "Sender · When": "Pelau House · Today, 9:42 a.m.", "Ti Kay · Mon 3 Aug, 9:30 p.m.". Use "Today" and weekday dates in the current week, "Sat 21 Nov" style otherwise, and lowercase "a.m./p.m." with periods.
  • System messages are one plain sentence naming who did what: "Demerara Kitchen can’t take on this event", "Marcia Thompson closed this enquiry", "Closed automatically 30 days after the event." A decline quotes the caterer's reason: “Fully booked that weekend.”
  • Help text says who sees what: "Pelau House gets an email. Links show as plain text." / "Priya sees Pelau House, not your email or phone."
  • Privacy notes stay short and concrete: "Pelau House sees your name, not your email or phone."
  • Messages are shown exactly as written, with emoji, Kreyòl, French and Spanish intact ("Ou se yon bon kizinyè. ¡Gracias!"). Links and markup show as plain text, never as live links or HTML.
  • Errors give the limit and the current count: "Keep your reply under 2,000 characters. It’s 2,143 now."

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Caterer-side messages had no visually hidden sender prefix; the customer side did. The system requires it on both.
  • The caterer side labelled the reply with .field__label, put the status outside .thread__title and the actions in an ad hoc .cluster with an inline gap; the system uses .reply-box__label, .thread__status and .thread__actions.
  • The failed message had no sender prefix and "Try again" was plain text; it is now a button.
  • The over-limit counter was coloured with an inline style in thread/invalid; it now uses data-over. The caterer-side invalid state dropped maxlength and the help text, and put the error after the counter; the system keeps the order error → help → counter.
  • "Sending…" was a live region on the caterer side only; it is role="status" on both. The caterer send button was both disabled and aria-busy; busy buttons use aria-disabled so focus is not lost.
  • Send was icon-only for customers but "Send" with text for caterers. Both are allowed; the icon-only form must keep aria-label="Send reply".
