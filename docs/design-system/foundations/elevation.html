Foundation

Elevation

Sweet Hand is flat. White space separates things on the page; shadows exist only to lift what floats above it: menus, toasts, dialogs and sheets.

Overview

The reference design has no card borders and no drop shadows: round photos sit straight on the white canvas. The system keeps that. Page content is flat at every viewport, and depth appears only when something genuinely sits above the page and must be told apart from it.

  • On the page: no shadow. Caterer cards have neither border nor shadow; data cards and the enquiry panel have a 1px --color-border-default hairline at most.
  • On hover: a soft level-2 lift on the primary button and interactive cards, as feedback, not structure.
  • Floating: menus, the area suggestions list, the filter dropdown, popovers and toasts use level 3.
  • Modal: dialogs, side sheets and drawers use level 4 over a backdrop.

Elevation has two parts: shadows (--shadow-*) say how far a surface floats, and z-order (--z-*) decides what covers what. They are separate scales because a toast (low shadow) must still sit above a dialog (high shadow).

Shadow levels

Four levels, each softer and wider than a typical material shadow so they read as lift rather than outline. All are tinted with --shadow-color, a warm stone (28 24 20) in light theme so shadows never look blue-grey against warm type.

Elevation in dark theme

A shadow is a darker area around a surface; on a near-black canvas there is almost nothing darker to show. Dark theme therefore does two things at once:

  1. Raised surfaces get lighter. Dialogs, sheets, menus and popovers use stone-850 against the stone-950 canvas, so the panel edge is visible without any shadow.
  2. Shadows turn black and stronger. --shadow-color becomes pure black (0 0 0) and opacities rise from 6–18% to 35–65%, enough to darken the area around a panel.

The backdrop also darkens from ink at 50% to black at 66%, so a dialog never floats over a page that looks the same as itself.

Z-order

Nine named layers with wide gaps between them. Components read the token; nobody writes z-index: 9999. A layer may use calc(var(--z-…) + 1) for a child that must sit above its own parent, never to jump to another layer.

TokenValueWhat sits there
--z-basePage content.
--z-raisedA selected segment in a button group, so its pepper edge covers its neighbours' borders; positioned decorations inside a component.
--z-stickyThe sticky top bar and the sticky enquiry bar on phones.
--z-dropdownMenus, the account menu, the filter dropdown.
--z-overlayThe backdrop behind a dialog or sheet.
--z-modalDialogs, sheets, drawers and the full-screen photo viewer.
--z-popoverPopovers, which may open from inside a dialog.
--z-toastThe toast region: a confirmation must stay visible over a dialog that triggered it.
--z-tooltipTooltips and the focused skip link: always on top.

Usage

  • No shadow on the page. If something is in the page flow, it does not get a shadow. Use space first, a hairline second.
  • Shadow follows the layer. Floating panels use level 3, modal panels level 4. Do not give a menu level 4 to make it "pop".
  • Raised surfaces read the raised role. Every floating panel uses --color-bg-surface-raised, never --color-bg-surface, so dark theme can lift it.
  • Hover lift is feedback. Level 2 appears on hover only, and only on things that are clickable as a whole.
  • One modal at a time. Do not stack a dialog on a dialog; replace the content or move to a screen. Popovers and toasts may sit above a dialog; that is what their layers are for.
  • Use the z tokens. No raw z-index numbers in component CSS.

Accessibility

  • Shadows are never the only edge. Dialog panels sit on a backdrop; menus, popovers and suggestions also have a 1px border. In forced-colours mode shadows disappear, and those borders become CanvasText, so every floating panel keeps a visible edge.
  • Focus not obscured (2.4.11). Sticky layers (100) never hide the focused element: the page reserves space for the sticky enquiry bar and scroll padding for the top bar. A focused element inside a dialog is never under the sticky dialog footer, because the footer is part of the scroll container.
  • Modal means inert. Content under the backdrop is inert and hidden from assistive technology while a dialog is open; z-order alone does not make something modal.
  • Toasts above dialogs must still not steal focus; they announce through a live region.
  • Hover lift moves 1px and is a transition that respects prefers-reduced-motion through the motion tokens.
